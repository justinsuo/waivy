#### 🍋 [Arnold Palmer](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-arnold-palmer/)
American · drink · easy  
**$0.83/serving** · 119 kcal · P1 · C32 · F0 · ⏱ 10 min · 🍽 1  

The classic golf-clubhouse refresher: equal parts cold black iced tea and fresh lemonade over ice. Bright, lightly sweet, and endlessly chuggable on a hot afternoon.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Black tea** — 1 bag steeped strong and chilled (about 6 oz brewed)
- **Lemon** — juiced, about 1 oz fresh juice
- **Sugar** — tsp, to taste; dissolve into the lemonade
- **Water** — oz cold water to make the lemonade
- **Ice** — cup, to fill the glass

**Steps**

1. Steep the black tea bag in 6 oz of hot water for 4-5 minutes, then remove the bag and chill the tea completely (or use pre-made unsweetened iced tea).
2. Make the lemonade: stir the fresh lemon juice and sugar into 5 oz cold water until the sugar fully dissolves.
3. Fill a tall glass to the top with ice.
4. Pour in the chilled tea and the lemonade in equal measure (about half and half), then stir gently to combine.
5. Taste and adjust with a touch more sugar or lemon if desired, and garnish with a lemon wheel.

</details>

#### 🍌 [Banana Protein Smoothie](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-protein-smoothie/)
American · drink · easy  
**$1.94/serving** · 540 kcal · P37 · C65 · F18 · ⏱ 3 min · 🍽 1  

A thick, creamy banana smoothie blended with vanilla protein powder and milk. Naturally sweet, packed with protein, and ready in about three minutes — the classic post-workout or grab-and-go breakfast drink.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Banana** — ripe; freeze ahead for a thicker, frostier blend
- **Protein powder** — 1 scoop, vanilla or unflavored
- **Milk** — about 1 cup
- **Peanut butter** — 1 tbsp for richness and extra protein _(optional)_
- **Honey** — 1 tsp, only if you want it sweeter _(optional)_
- **Ice** — 1/2 cup, for a colder, thicker texture
- **Cinnamon** — a pinch, for warmth _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Peel the banana and break it into a few chunks, then add it to the blender.
2. Add the protein powder, milk, peanut butter and honey (if using), and the ice.
3. Blend on high for 30-45 seconds, until completely smooth and frothy with no banana lumps.
4. Check the consistency: add a splash more milk to thin it out, or a few more ice cubes to thicken.
5. Pour into a tall glass, dust with a pinch of cinnamon, and drink right away.

</details>

#### 🍅 [Bloody Mary](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-bloody-mary/)
American · drink · easy  
**$2.47/serving** · 36 kcal · P1 · C9 · F0 · ⏱ 5 min · 🍽 1  

The definitive brunch cocktail: chilled vodka and tomato juice built tall over ice with a savory, spicy backbone of Worcestershire, hot sauce, lemon, and celery salt. Stirred (never shaken hard) to keep it silky, then garnished with a crisp celery stalk and a lime wedge.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Vodka** — 2 oz
- **Tomato juice** — 4 oz
- **Lemon** — freshly squeezed, about 0.5 oz juice
- **Worcestershire sauce** — 2 dashes
- **Hot sauce** — 3-4 dashes, to taste
- **Celery salt** — plus extra for rimming the glass
- **Black pepper** — a few cracks
- **Ice** — 1 cup
- **Celery** — 1 inner stalk with leaves, for garnish
- **Lime** — wedge for garnish and rim _(optional)_
- **Prepared horseradish** — prepared, for extra bite _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Rub a lime wedge around the rim of a tall glass, then dip the rim in celery salt to coat. Fill the glass with ice.
2. In a mixing glass or shaker, combine the vodka, tomato juice, fresh lemon juice, Worcestershire, hot sauce, celery salt, black pepper, and horseradish if using.
3. Add a scoop of ice and roll the drink: gently pour it back and forth between the shaker and mixing glass 3-4 times to mix and chill without over-diluting.
4. Strain into the prepared ice-filled glass.
5. Garnish with the celery stalk and a lime wedge. Add an extra crack of black pepper on top and serve immediately.

</details>

#### 🍓 [Classic Strawberry Banana Smoothie](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-strawberry-banana-smoothie/)
American · drink · easy  
**$2.63/serving** · 359 kcal · P17 · C67 · F5 · ⏱ 5 min · 🍽 1  

A thick, creamy blend of ripe strawberries and banana with Greek yogurt and a splash of milk. Naturally sweet, packed with protein, and ready in under five minutes.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Strawberries** — about 1 cup, fresh or frozen
- **Banana** — ripe; freeze for a thicker blend
- **Greek yogurt** — 1/2 cup plain or vanilla
- **Milk** — 1/2 cup; add more to thin
- **Honey** — 1 tsp, to taste _(optional)_
- **Ice** — 1/2 cup if using fresh fruit _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the milk to the blender first, then the Greek yogurt, banana, and strawberries so the blade catches the liquid.
2. Drizzle in the honey and add ice if you are using fresh (not frozen) fruit.
3. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy, stopping to scrape down the sides if needed.
4. Check the consistency: add a splash more milk to thin it out or a few more frozen berries to thicken.
5. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.

</details>

#### 🍸 [Cosmopolitan](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-cosmopolitan/)
American · drink · easy  
**$1.84/serving** · 107 kcal · P0 · C20 · F0 · ⏱ 4 min · 🍽 1  

The iconic pink cocktail: citrus vodka, triple sec, tart cranberry, and fresh lime shaken ice-cold and served up. Crisp, dry, and elegant with a flamed orange or lime twist.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Vodka** — citrus vodka if you have it
- **Triple sec** — Cointreau is ideal
- **Cranberry juice** — use real cranberry, not cocktail blend
- **Lime** — freshly squeezed, about 0.5 oz juice
- **Simple syrup** — only if you like it less tart _(optional)_
- **Ice** — for shaking

**Steps**

1. Chill a coupe or martini glass by filling it with ice and water while you build the drink.
2. Add the vodka, triple sec, cranberry juice, fresh lime juice, and optional simple syrup to a cocktail shaker.
3. Fill the shaker with ice and shake hard for 12-15 seconds until the outside is frosted.
4. Empty the chilled glass and double-strain the cocktail into it to keep it crystal clear.
5. Garnish with a lime wheel or a flamed orange twist and serve immediately.

</details>

Classic fresh-squeezed lemonade — bright, tart, and just sweet enough. Real lemon juice, a quick sugar dissolve, and cold water over ice. The only summer drink that actually tastes like summer.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Lemon** — for about 3 oz fresh juice
- **Sugar** — 2 tbsp, adjust to taste
- **Water** — cold, still or sparkling
- **Ice** — 1 cup, to fill the glass
- **Fresh mint** — sprig, for garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Roll the lemons firmly on the counter, then halve and squeeze to get about 3 oz (1/3 cup) fresh juice. Strain out the seeds.
2. In your serving glass, stir the sugar into the lemon juice until fully dissolved — this makes an instant syrup with no cooking needed.
3. Pour in the cold water and stir well to combine. Taste and add a splash more sugar or water to balance tart and sweet.
4. Fill the glass with ice, give it one last stir, and garnish with a mint sprig or a thin lemon wheel.

</details>

A bright, naturally sweet blend of fresh spinach, ripe banana, and frozen mango with creamy oat milk. The fruit completely tames the greens, so it tastes like a tropical fruit smoothie while sneaking in a full serving of leafy vegetables.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Spinach** — about 2 packed cups fresh
- **Banana** — ripe, ideally frozen in chunks
- **Frozen mango** — about 3/4 cup chunks
- **Oat milk** — 1 cup, plus more to thin
- **Honey** — 1 tsp, only if fruit isn't sweet enough _(optional)_
- **Chia seeds** — 1 tsp for fiber and body _(optional)_

1. Add the oat milk to the blender first, then load in the spinach, banana, and frozen mango.
2. Add the optional honey, chia seeds, and ice if your fruit isn't frozen.
3. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds, until completely smooth with no flecks of spinach remaining.
4. Check consistency and splash in a little more oat milk to thin if needed, then pulse once.
5. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately while cold and frothy.

</details>

#### 🥃 [Old Fashioned](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-old-fashioned/)
American · drink · easy  
**$3.18/serving** · 258 kcal · P1 · C28 · F0 · ⏱ 4 min · 🍽 1  

The original whiskey cocktail: bourbon gently stirred with sugar and aromatic bitters over a big rock of ice, finished with an expressed orange peel. Spirit-forward, smooth, and timeless.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Bourbon** — 2 oz, the backbone of the drink
- **Sugar** — 1 sugar cube, or 1/2 tsp
- **Aromatic bitters** — 2-3 dashes Angostura aromatic bitters
- **Ice** — 1 large cube preferred
- **Orange** — 1 wide peel, for expressing and garnish
- **Fresh cherries** — or a brandied/Luxardo cherry, for garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the sugar cube to a rocks glass and saturate it with 2-3 dashes of bitters plus a small splash of water. Muddle until the sugar mostly dissolves into a paste.
2. Pour in the bourbon and add one large ice cube. Stir gently for 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute.
3. Express the orange peel over the glass by pinching it skin-side down to release the oils, then rub it around the rim and drop it in.
4. Garnish with a cherry if using, and serve immediately.

</details>

Smooth, low-acid cold brew coffee sweetened with vanilla and softened with a splash of milk. Poured over ice for a refreshing, café-style pick-me-up you can build in under two minutes.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Cold brew concentrate** — chilled, undiluted cold brew concentrate or ready-to-drink
- **Ice** — cubes, fills the glass
- **Milk** — splash; whole or 2% for creaminess

- **Milk** — cold; whole or 2% for creamiest result
- **Honey** — or to taste; whisk into the matcha while warm-ish _(optional)_
- **Ice** — fills the glass
- **Vanilla extract** — optional flavor lift _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Sift 2 tsp matcha into a small bowl or measuring cup to break up clumps. Add about 2 oz hot (not boiling, ~175F) or room-temp water along with the honey.
2. Whisk vigorously in a zig-zag W motion with a bamboo whisk or small electric frother for 20-30 seconds until smooth and frothy with no lumps.
3. Fill a tall glass with ice and pour in 8 oz cold milk. Add the vanilla extract if using.
4. Slowly pour the whisked matcha over the milk so it layers into the glass.
5. Stir well before drinking to combine, and enjoy cold.

</details>

#### 🍹 [Classic Margarita](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-margarita/)
Mexican · drink · easy

The definitive tequila cocktail: 100% agave tequila, fresh lime juice, and orange liqueur shaken cold and served over ice in a salt-rimmed glass. Bright, tart, and perfectly balanced.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Tequila** — blanco/silver, 100% agave
- **Triple sec** — or Cointreau
- **Lime** — juiced, about 1 oz fresh lime juice
- **Agave syrup** — to taste, for a touch of sweetness _(optional)_
- **Ice** — for shaking and serving
- **Salt** — for the rim _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Run a lime wedge around the outer rim of a rocks glass, then dip the rim in salt spread on a small plate. Fill the glass with fresh ice.
2. Add the tequila, triple sec, fresh lime juice, and agave syrup to a cocktail shaker.
3. Fill the shaker with ice and shake hard for 15-20 seconds until well chilled and frothy.
4. Strain into the prepared salt-rimmed glass over fresh ice.
5. Garnish with a lime wheel and serve immediately.

</details>

#### 🍹 [Mai Tai](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-mai-tai/)
Tiki · drink · easy  
**$2.51/serving** · 261 kcal · P1 · C22 · F0 · ⏱ 4 min · 🍽 1  

The classic tiki cocktail: aged white rum shaken with fresh lime, orange triple sec and a touch of almond-sweetened syrup, served over crushed ice with a dark rum float and a mint sprig.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **White rum** — aged white or gold rum, the base
- **Rum** — dark rum for the float on top
- **Lime** — freshly squeezed, about 0.75 oz
- **Triple sec** — orange curaçao
- **Simple syrup** — stands in for orgeat
- **Almond extract** — a tiny drop to mimic orgeat's almond note _(optional)_
- **Ice** — crushed or cubed
- **Fresh mint** — sprig, to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Squeeze half a lime to get about 0.75 oz of fresh juice; reserve the spent shell.
2. Add the white rum, lime juice, triple sec, simple syrup and almond extract to a shaker with a scoop of ice.
3. Shake hard for 10-12 seconds until well-chilled and frothy.
4. Fill a rocks or tiki glass with crushed ice and strain the cocktail over the top.
5. Gently float the dark rum over the back of a bar spoon so it pools on the surface.
6. Garnish with the spent lime shell and a slapped sprit of mint, then serve with a straw.

</details>

#### 🥭 [Mango Smoothie](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-mango-smoothie/)
Tropical · drink · easy  
**$3.90/serving** · 392 kcal · P19 · C68 · F6 · ⏱ 5 min · 🍽 1  

A thick, creamy mango smoothie blended with Greek yogurt and milk — naturally sweet, tropical, and ready in under five minutes. No stovetop, no fuss, just frozen mango whirled into a frosty, protein-packed glass.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Frozen mango** — cups, frozen for a thick frosty texture
- **Greek yogurt** — cup plain, for creaminess and protein
- **Milk** — cup, to blend (use more to thin)
- **Honey** — tbsp, to taste depending on mango sweetness _(optional)_
- **Ice** — cup, only if mango is not frozen _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the frozen mango, Greek yogurt, and milk to the blender, layering the liquid in first so the blades catch.
2. Drizzle in the honey if using, and toss in the ice only if your mango isn't already frozen.
3. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds, stopping to scrape down the sides, until completely smooth and creamy.
4. Check the texture: add a splash more milk to loosen, or a few more frozen mango chunks to thicken.
5. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately while frosty.

</details>

#### 🥭 [Tropical Sunrise Smoothie](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/drink-tropical-smoothie/)

A bright, creamy blend of mango, pineapple, and banana with a splash of orange juice and Greek yogurt for body. Tastes like a beach vacation in a glass — naturally sweet, no added sugar needed.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Mango** — fresh or frozen chunks, about 3/4 cup
- **Pineapple** — fresh or frozen chunks, about 1/2 cup
- **Banana** — ripe, peeled; freeze ahead for a thicker smoothie
- **Orange juice** — about 1/2 cup as the liquid base
- **Greek yogurt** — about 1/3 cup plain, for creaminess and protein
- **Ice** — skip if using frozen fruit _(optional)_
- **Honey** — only if your fruit isn't very ripe _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the orange juice and Greek yogurt to the blender first so the blades catch them easily.
2. Add the mango, pineapple, banana, and ice (skip the ice if your fruit is frozen).
3. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides if needed.
4. Taste and blend in the honey only if it needs more sweetness; add a splash more orange juice to thin it out.
5. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately while cold and frothy.

</details>
