#### 🥪 [Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Sandwich](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-breakfast-sandwich/)
American · breakfast · easy  
**$2.26/serving** · 332 kcal · P21 · C28 · F15 · ⏱ 15 min · 🍽 2  

The deli-counter classic done right: a folded, custardy fried egg layered with crisp bacon and melty American cheese on a buttered, griddle-toasted English muffin. Salty, rich, and ready in about ten minutes.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **English muffin** — 2 muffin
- **Eggs** — 4 egg
- **Bacon** — 4 strips total, 2 per sandwich
- **American cheese singles** — 1 slice per sandwich
- **Butter** — for toasting the muffins
- **Salt** — 0.01 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.01 tsp
- **Hot sauce** — a few dashes, to taste _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Split the English muffins and toast them, cut-side down, in a dry or lightly buttered skillet over medium heat until golden; set aside.
2. In the same skillet over medium heat, lay the bacon strips and cook, turning once, until crisp, about 6 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel and pour off all but a thin film of fat.
3. Crack the eggs into the pan, season with salt and pepper, and break the yolks. Cook until the whites set on the bottom, about 1 minute.
4. Fold each egg over on itself into a muffin-sized square, then flip and cook 30 seconds more so the yolk stays just set and custardy.
5. Lay a slice of American cheese over the hot eggs and let it melt for 20 seconds; off the heat, the residual warmth finishes the melt.
6. Build each sandwich: bottom muffin, the cheese-topped egg, two strips of bacon snapped to fit, a few dashes of hot sauce if using, then the top muffin.
7. Press gently, wrap in foil or parchment for a minute to let everything meld, then serve warm.

</details>

#### 🍮 [Bread Pudding](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-bread-pudding/)
American · snack · easy  
**$0.31/serving** · 61 kcal · P4 · C4 · F3 · ⏱ 90 min · 🍽 8  

Old-fashioned baked bread pudding: cubes of day-old bread soaked in a rich vanilla custard spiced with cinnamon and nutmeg, studded with raisins, and baked until the top is golden and crisp while the inside stays soft and creamy. Finished with a warm bourbon-butter sauce.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Bread** — about 10 cups day-old bread cubes; brioche or challah is ideal
- **Eggs** — 4 egg
- **Whole milk** — 2 cups
- **Heavy cream** — 1 cup, for custard plus sauce
- **Sugar** — 3/4 cup granulated
- **Brown sugar** — 1/2 cup, packed, for sauce
- **Butter** — 6 tbsp total: 2 for dish, 4 for sauce
- **Vanilla extract** — 2 tsp
- **Cinnamon** — 1.5 tsp
- **Nutmeg** — 1/4 tsp, freshly grated
- **Salt** — 1/2 tsp
- **Raisins** — 3/4 cup _(optional)_
- **Bourbon** — 2 tbsp for the sauce _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Cut the bread into 1-inch cubes and spread on a sheet pan. If the bread is fresh, dry it in a 300F oven for 10-15 minutes until lightly toasted but not browned; day-old bread can be used as is. Butter a 9x13 baking dish with about 2 tbsp of the butter.
2. Soak the raisins in a splash of warm water (or a little of the bourbon) for 10 minutes to plump, then drain. Pile the bread cubes into the buttered dish and scatter the raisins throughout.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with the granulated sugar until smooth. Whisk in the whole milk, about 3/4 cup of the heavy cream, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt until fully combined.
4. Pour the custard evenly over the bread. Press the bread down with your hands or a spatula so every cube is submerged, then let it soak at least 20-30 minutes (or cover and refrigerate up to overnight), pressing once or twice so the bread fully absorbs the custard.
5. Heat the oven to 350F. Bake the pudding uncovered for 45-55 minutes, until the top is golden and puffed and the center is set with just a slight jiggle; a knife inserted off-center should come out clean.
6. While it bakes, make the sauce: melt the remaining 4 tbsp butter in a small saucepan, stir in the brown sugar and remaining heavy cream, and simmer 2-3 minutes until smooth and slightly thickened. Off the heat, stir in the bourbon.
7. Let the bread pudding rest 10-15 minutes so it sets up. Spoon the warm bourbon-butter sauce over individual servings and serve warm.

</details>

#### 🥦 [Broccoli Cheddar Soup](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-broccoli-cheddar-soup/)
American · lunch · easy  
**$0.71/serving** · 55 kcal · P2 · C10 · F1 · ⏱ 40 min · 🍽 4  

A velvety, deeply cheesy bowl built on a proper butter-and-flour roux, simmered with tender broccoli and shredded carrot, then finished off the heat with sharp cheddar so it melts silky instead of grainy. Better than the bread-bowl version you're thinking of.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Butter** — 4 tbsp
- **Onion** — 1 medium, finely diced
- **Garlic** — 2 cloves, minced
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1/4 cup, for the roux
- **Chicken broth** — about 2 cups; use veggie-broth for vegetarian
- **Half and half** — about 2 cups; or whole milk for lighter
- **Broccoli** — 2 heads / ~4 cups small florets, stems chopped
- **Carrot** — 1.5 carrots, grated or julienned
- **Sharp cheddar block** — about 8 oz sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
- **Nutmeg** — tiny pinch, freshly grated
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.02 tsp
- **Dijon mustard** — 1 tsp, brightens the cheese _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Melt the butter in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion with a pinch of salt and cook 4-5 minutes until soft and translucent, then add the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Sprinkle in the flour and stir constantly for 2-3 minutes to cook out the raw taste, forming a blond roux that coats the onions.
3. Slowly whisk in the broth a splash at a time so no lumps form, then whisk in the half-and-half. Bring to a gentle simmer, whisking, until slightly thickened.
4. Add the broccoli florets and chopped stems plus the grated carrot. Simmer uncovered 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the broccoli is very tender.
5. For a smoother soup, blend about a third with an immersion blender (or mash some florets against the pot); leave the rest chunky for texture.
6. Reduce the heat to low. Grate in the nutmeg and stir in the optional Dijon. Add the shredded cheddar a large handful at a time, stirring until each addition melts before adding more.
7. Once all the cheese is in and the soup is glossy, taste and season with salt and plenty of black pepper. Do not let it boil after the cheese goes in or it will turn grainy.
8. Ladle into bowls and serve hot, topped with extra cheddar and cracked pepper.

</details>

#### 🍞 [Cinnamon French Toast](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-cinnamon-french-toast/)
American · breakfast · easy  
**$0.66/serving** · 103 kcal · P7 · C3 · F7 · ⏱ 25 min · 🍽 4  

Thick slices of brioche soaked in a vanilla-cinnamon custard and griddled in butter until golden and crisp outside, soft and creamy inside. Dusted with cinnamon sugar and finished with real maple syrup.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Brioche buns** — 8 thick slices of a brioche or challah loaf
- **Eggs** — 4 egg
- **Milk** — about 1 cup whole milk
- **Vanilla extract** — 2 tsp vanilla extract
- **Cinnamon** — 1.5 tsp for custard plus extra for cinnamon sugar
- **Sugar** — 2 tbsp in custard plus more for dusting
- **Salt** — pinch
- **Butter** — for the griddle, ~3 tbsp
- **Maple syrup** — for serving
- **Nutmeg** — tiny grating, optional _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Slice the brioche or challah into eight thick (about 3/4-inch) slices. If the bread is fresh, leave the slices out for 10-15 minutes so they dry slightly and soak up custard without falling apart.
2. In a wide shallow bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla, 1.5 tsp cinnamon, 2 tbsp sugar, the pinch of salt, and the optional nutmeg until fully smooth with no streaks of egg white.
3. In a small dish, stir together 2 tbsp sugar with 1/2 tsp cinnamon to make cinnamon sugar for finishing.
4. Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat and melt about 1 tbsp butter until foaming but not browned.
5. Dip each slice into the custard for 15-20 seconds per side, letting it soak through but not turn soggy, then let excess drip off.
6. Griddle the slices 2-3 minutes per side until deep golden brown and set in the center, adding fresh butter between batches and lowering the heat if they brown too fast.
7. Transfer to a plate, dust both sides with the cinnamon sugar while still hot, and serve immediately with warm maple syrup.

</details>

#### 🥧 [Classic Apple Pie](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-classic-apple-pie/)
American · snack · medium  
**$1.01/serving** · 150 kcal · P2 · C32 · F2 · ⏱ 300 min · 🍽 8  

A proper double-crust apple pie: flaky all-butter pastry around a mound of tart-sweet apples spiced with cinnamon and nutmeg, thickened just enough so it slices clean. Egg-washed and sprinkled with coarse sugar for a crackly, golden top.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — about 2.5 cups for the double crust
- **Butter** — 1 cup (2 sticks) cold, cubed, for the crust
- **Salt** — 1 tsp in crust, plus a pinch in filling
- **Sugar** — 1 Tbsp in crust + 1/2 cup in filling
- **Apple** — about 3 lb / 8 apples, mix of Granny Smith and Honeycrisp
- **Brown sugar** — 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- **Cornstarch** — 3 Tbsp to thicken the juices
- **Cinnamon** — 1.5 tsp ground
- **Nutmeg** — 1/4 tsp freshly grated
- **Lemon** — 1 Tbsp juice to brighten and keep apples from browning
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Eggs** — 1 egg beaten with a splash of water for the wash
- **Turbinado sugar** — 1 Tbsp coarse sugar for the top _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make the crust: whisk flour, 1 tsp salt, and 1 Tbsp sugar, then cut in the cold cubed butter with your fingers or a pastry cutter until pea-sized pieces remain. Drizzle in ice water a tablespoon at a time, tossing just until the dough holds when pressed. Divide in two, flatten into disks, wrap, and chill at least 1 hour.
2. Make the filling: peel, core, and slice the apples 1/4-inch thick. Toss with the 1/2 cup sugar, brown sugar, cornstarch, cinnamon, nutmeg, a pinch of salt, lemon juice, and vanilla. Let sit 15-20 minutes so the apples release some juice.
3. Roll the first disk on a floured surface into a 12-inch round and fit it into a 9-inch pie plate, letting the edges overhang. Roll the second disk into another 12-inch round for the top.
4. Pile the apples into the shell, mounding slightly in the center, and pour over the juices that collected in the bowl. Dot the top with a few small pieces of butter if any remain.
5. Lay the top crust over the apples (or weave a lattice). Trim to a 1-inch overhang, fold the edges under, and crimp to seal. Cut a few steam vents in a solid top crust.
6. Brush the crust with egg wash and sprinkle with turbinado sugar. Chill the assembled pie 20 minutes while the oven heats to 425F (220C).
7. Bake at 425F for 20 minutes, then lower to 375F (190C) and bake 40-50 minutes more, until the crust is deep golden and you see thick juices bubbling at the vents. Tent the edges with foil if they brown too fast.
8. Cool the pie on a rack at least 3-4 hours before slicing so the filling sets; cutting too early gives you a runny pie.

</details>

#### 🍌 [Classic Banana Bread](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-classic-banana-bread/)
American · snack · easy  
**$0.23/serving** · 91 kcal · P3 · C16 · F3 · ⏱ 80 min · 🍽 10  

A moist, tender loaf built on very ripe, spotty bananas, brown sugar, and melted butter for deep caramel flavor. Mixed by hand in one bowl, it bakes up with a crackly top and a soft, fragrant crumb.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Banana** — very ripe, heavily spotted, mashed (about 1.5 cups)
- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose, ~2 cups (250g)
- **Brown sugar** — packed light brown, ~3/4 cup
- **White sugar** — ~1/4 cup, for a crackly top
- **Butter** — unsalted, melted and cooled, ~1/2 cup (113g)
- **Eggs** — large, room temperature
- **Vanilla extract** — 2 tsp
- **Baking soda** — 1 tsp
- **Salt** — 1/2 tsp
- **Cinnamon** — 1/2 tsp
- **Sour cream** — 1/4 cup, for extra moisture _(optional)_
- **Walnuts** — 3/4 cup, toasted and chopped _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Heat the oven to 350F (175C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan and line it with a parchment sling so the loaf lifts out cleanly.
2. In a large bowl, mash the very ripe bananas with a fork until mostly smooth with a few small lumps. The riper and spottier the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread.
3. Whisk the melted butter, brown sugar, and white sugar into the mashed bananas, then beat in the eggs, vanilla, and sour cream until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
4. Sprinkle the baking soda, salt, and cinnamon over the wet mixture and stir to distribute evenly before adding the flour.
5. Add the flour and fold gently with a spatula just until no dry streaks remain; stop mixing the moment it comes together to keep the crumb tender. Fold in the toasted walnuts if using.
6. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan, smooth the top, and tap the pan once on the counter to release large air pockets.
7. Bake 55 to 65 minutes, until the top is deep golden, cracked down the center, and a toothpick inserted in the middle comes out with only a few moist crumbs. Tent loosely with foil if it browns too fast.
8. Cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then lift out using the parchment and cool on a rack at least 30 minutes before slicing so the crumb sets.

</details>

#### 🍖 [Classic Meatloaf](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-classic-meatloaf/)
American · dinner · easy  
**$1.93/serving** · 330 kcal · P25 · C4 · F24 · ⏱ 90 min · 🍽 6  

A tender, savory all-American meatloaf built on a milk-soaked bread panade so it never dries out, seasoned with sauteed onion and garlic, and finished with a sweet-tangy ketchup-brown sugar glaze that caramelizes in the oven. Comfort food done right.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Ground beef** — 80/20 ground beef, ~2 lb total
- **Bread** — 2 slices white bread, torn, for the panade
- **Milk** — 1/3 cup to soak the bread
- **Eggs** — binder
- **Onion** — finely diced and sauteed
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Worcestershire sauce** — 2 tbsp, for savory depth
- **Ketchup** — 2 tbsp in the mix + the rest for the glaze
- **Fresh parsley** — 2 tbsp chopped
- **Olive oil** — to saute the onion
- **Salt** — 0.05 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.03 tsp
- **Brown sugar** — 2 tbsp for the glaze
- **Dijon mustard** — 1 tsp in the glaze for tang _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Preheat the oven to 350F (175C) and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil or lightly oil a loaf pan. Tear the bread into small pieces, place in a large bowl, pour the milk over it, and let it soak for 5 minutes until soft.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and saute the diced onion for 6-8 minutes until soft and golden. Add the minced garlic and cook 1 minute more, then let it cool slightly so it doesn't cook the eggs.
3. Mash the soaked bread into a paste, then add the eggs, Worcestershire, 2 tbsp ketchup, chopped parsley, salt, pepper, and the cooled onion mixture. Whisk together until evenly combined.
4. Add the ground beef and mix gently with your hands just until combined. Do not overwork it or the loaf will turn dense and rubbery.
5. Transfer the mixture to the baking sheet and shape it into a free-form loaf about 9 by 5 inches (or press into the loaf pan). A free-form loaf gives more caramelized surface.
6. Stir together the remaining ketchup, brown sugar, and Dijon to make the glaze. Spread half of it over the top of the loaf.
7. Bake for 40 minutes, then brush on the remaining glaze and bake 15-25 minutes more, until the internal temperature reaches 160F (71C) on a thermometer.
8. Rest the meatloaf for 10 minutes before slicing so the juices redistribute, then cut into thick slices and serve.

</details>

#### 🍳 [Crispy Breakfast Hash](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-breakfast-hash/)
American · breakfast · medium  
**$3.04/serving** · 606 kcal · P25 · C44 · F37 · ⏱ 40 min · 🍽 4  

Golden, crusty diced potatoes pan-fried with sweet onion, bell pepper, and browned breakfast sausage, then crowned with runny fried eggs and a hit of hot sauce. The secret is patience: spread the potatoes in a single layer and let them sit so they build a real crust before you stir.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Potato** — about 1.5 lb russet or Yukon Gold, diced into 1/2-inch cubes
- **Pork sausage** — breakfast sausage, casings removed if links
- **Eggs** — 4 egg
- **Onion** — 1 onion
- **Bell pepper** — 1 pepper
- **Garlic** — 2 cloves, minced
- **Olive oil** — tablespoons, plus a little more if needed
- **Smoked paprika** — teaspoon
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Black pepper** — to taste
- **Scallion** — sliced, for garnish _(optional)_
- **Hot sauce** — for serving _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Dice the potatoes into even 1/2-inch cubes. Cover with cold water in a pot, add a big pinch of salt, bring to a boil, and parboil for 5 minutes until just barely tender. Drain well and let steam-dry for a couple minutes so the surfaces are dry.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large cast-iron or heavy nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add the sausage, breaking it into bite-size crumbles, and cook 4 to 5 minutes until browned. Scoop it out with a slotted spoon, leaving the rendered fat in the pan.
3. Add the parboiled potatoes to the hot fat and spread them into a single layer. Let them cook undisturbed for 4 to 5 minutes so a golden crust forms, then flip and repeat, seasoning with the smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Total 12 to 15 minutes until deeply crispy.
4. Push the potatoes to one side and add the diced onion and bell pepper to the open space. Cook 4 to 5 minutes until softened and lightly charred, then stir the minced garlic through for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Return the browned sausage to the pan and fold everything together. Taste and adjust salt and pepper, then press the hash into an even layer and let it sit one more minute to re-crisp the bottom.
6. Crack the eggs into a separate oiled skillet (or clear spots in the hash) and fry over medium heat until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny, about 3 minutes.
7. Divide the hash among plates, top each with a fried egg, and finish with sliced scallions and a few dashes of hot sauce. Serve immediately while the egg is hot and the potatoes are crisp.

</details>

#### 🥔 [Crispy Hash Browns](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-crispy-hash-browns/)
American · breakfast · medium  
**$0.46/serving** · 166 kcal · P4 · C37 · F1 · ⏱ 30 min · 🍽 4  

Golden, shatteringly crisp hash browns made from freshly grated russet potatoes wrung bone-dry, then pan-fried in butter and oil until deeply browned with a fluffy interior. The trick is removing every drop of moisture and not touching the potatoes until a crust forms.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Potato** — russet potatoes, peeled and grated
- **Butter** — 2 tbsp, for browning and flavor
- **Vegetable oil** — 2 tbsp, high smoke point for the crust
- **Salt** — 0.04 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.02 tsp
- **Onion powder** — 0.03 tsp _(optional)_
- **Garlic powder** — 0.03 tsp _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Peel the russet potatoes and grate them on the large holes of a box grater into a bowl of cold water; swish to release starch.
2. Drain, then transfer the grated potato to a clean kitchen towel. Wring hard over the sink until the potatoes are as dry as possible, removing every drop of water, then fluff them loose.
3. Toss the squeezed potatoes with salt, pepper, and the optional onion and garlic powder so they season evenly.
4. Heat a heavy nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add the vegetable oil and half the butter and let it foam.
5. Add the potatoes and press into an even 1/2-inch layer with a spatula, leaving small gaps so steam escapes. Do not stir.
6. Cook undisturbed 5-7 minutes until the underside is deep golden brown and crisp; resist the urge to flip early.
7. Add the remaining butter around the edges, then flip in sections (or as one cake) and cook another 4-6 minutes until the second side is browned and the center is tender.
8. Drain on a paper towel briefly, season with a final pinch of salt, and serve immediately while crisp.

</details>

#### 🍫 [Fudgy Brownies](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-fudgy-brownies/)
American · snack · easy  
**$0.16/serving** · 30 kcal · P2 · C2 · F2 · ⏱ 95 min · 🍽 12  

Dense, deeply chocolatey brownies with a glossy crackly top and a moist, almost-underbaked center. A high butter-and-chocolate ratio plus minimal flour gives them that signature fudgy chew — no cakey crumb in sight.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Butter** — 1/2 cup (1 stick), melted
- **Dark chocolate** — 4 oz semi-sweet/dark chocolate, chopped
- **Sugar** — 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- **Brown sugar** — 1/4 cup packed, for extra chew
- **Eggs** — large, room temperature
- **Vanilla extract** — 2 tsp vanilla extract
- **Cocoa powder** — 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- **All-purpose flour** — 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
- **Salt** — 3/4 tsp fine salt
- **Chocolate chips** — 1/2 cup, folded in _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Heat oven to 350F (175C). Line an 8x8-inch metal pan with parchment, leaving overhang, and lightly grease.
2. In a heatproof bowl, combine the melted butter and chopped dark chocolate. Microwave in 20-second bursts, stirring between each, until smooth and fully melted, then let cool slightly.
3. Whisk both sugars into the warm chocolate mixture. Add the eggs and vanilla and whisk vigorously for a full minute until the batter is glossy and slightly thickened — this beating is what creates the shiny crackly top.
4. Sift the cocoa powder, flour, and salt over the wet mixture. Fold gently with a spatula just until no dry streaks remain; do not overmix or the brownies turn cakey.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips if using. Scrape the thick batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Beef stew meat** — chuck, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1/3 cup, for dredging
- **Olive oil** — about 3 tbsp, for searing
- **Onion** — 2 onion
- **Carrot** — 4 carrot

- **Onion** — large yellow onion, diced
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Jalapeño** — seeded and minced
- **Tomato paste** — about 3 tbsp
- **Crushed tomatoes** — 28 oz can
- **Beef broth** — about 1.5 cups
- **Kidney beans (canned)** — drained and rinsed
- **Pinto beans (canned)** — drained and rinsed
- **Chili powder** — tbsp; the backbone
- **Ancho chili powder** — tbsp for fruity depth
- **Cumin** — tbsp, ground
- **Smoked paprika** — tsp
- **Oregano** — tsp dried, Mexican if you have it
- **Chipotle peppers in adobo** — 1 chile minced + 1 tsp sauce, optional _(optional)_
- **Olive oil** — tbsp
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Black pepper** — to taste
- **Shredded cheese** — shredded cheddar, to serve _(optional)_
- **Sour cream** — to serve _(optional)_
- **Scallion** — sliced, to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Heat the olive oil in a heavy Dutch oven or wide pot over medium-high. Add the ground beef in a single layer and let it sit undisturbed for 3-4 minutes to build a brown crust, then break it up and finish browning. Season with salt and pepper. Spoon off all but about a tablespoon of fat.
2. Lower the heat to medium and add the onion and jalapeño to the beef. Cook for 5-6 minutes until softened, then stir in the garlic and cook 1 minute more until fragrant.
3. Push the meat aside and add the tomato paste, chili powder, ancho chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano directly into the hot fat. Toast, stirring constantly, for 60-90 seconds until the spices darken and smell nutty — this blooming step is what separates real chili from a bland one.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and beef broth, scraping up every browned bit stuck to the bottom of the pot. Add the chipotle in adobo if using.
5. Bring to a gentle bubble, then reduce to low. Partially cover and simmer for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, letting the flavors meld and the liquid reduce.
6. Stir in the drained kidney beans and pinto beans. Continue simmering uncovered for another 20-30 minutes, until the chili is thick enough to mound on a spoon.
7. Taste and adjust: more salt for roundness, a pinch more chili powder for heat, or a splash of broth if it's too tight. Let it rest off the heat for 10 minutes before serving.
8. Ladle into bowls and top with shredded cheddar, a dollop of sour cream, and sliced scallions.

3. Add the beaten egg to the marinated chicken, then add about 1/2 cup cornstarch a little at a time, tossing until each piece is coated in a thick, dry-ish paste that clings.
4. Heat 1 inch of vegetable oil in a wok or deep skillet to 350F. Fry the chicken in 2-3 batches, 4-5 minutes per batch, until golden and crisp; drain on a rack. Optionally raise heat to 375F and flash-fry everything 60 seconds more for extra crunch.
5. Pour off all but 1 tbsp oil. Over medium heat, add the dried chiles and toast 20-30 seconds until fragrant and a shade darker (don't let them burn black).
6. Add the minced garlic, ginger, and scallion whites; stir-fry 30 seconds until aromatic.
7. Give the sauce a quick re-stir and pour it in. Bring to a simmer and cook 1-2 minutes until it turns glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.
8. Add the fried chicken and toss quickly to coat every piece, about 30 seconds, just until the sauce clings and the chicken is heated through.
9. Kill the heat, drizzle with toasted sesame oil, and toss with the scallion greens. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve immediately over steamed rice.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Pork chop** — boneless pork loin chops, pounded to ~1/4 inch
- **All-purpose flour** — about 3/4 cup for dredging

- **Butter** — a knob added to the frying oil at the end for flavor _(optional)_
- **Fresh parsley** — chopped, to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

#### 🍆 [Beef Moussaka](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-beef-moussaka/)
Greek · dinner · hard  
**$3.71/serving** · 453 kcal · P28 · C27 · F26 · ⏱ 150 min · 🍽 6  

The classic Greek baked casserole: layers of roasted eggplant and potato over a cinnamon-and-allspice-scented beef tomato sauce, crowned with a thick, creamy béchamel that bakes into a golden, custardy top. Rich and deeply comforting, it's even better the next day.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Eggplant** — large globe eggplants, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
- **Potato** — starchy potatoes, peeled and sliced into 1/4-inch rounds
- **Ground beef** — 80/20 ground beef for the meat sauce
- **Onion** — finely diced
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Tomato paste** — 2 tbsp
- **Crushed tomatoes** — one 14 oz can
- **Red wine (cooking)** — about 1/2 cup dry red wine
- **Cinnamon** — 1 tsp ground, plus a stick if available
- **Allspice** — 1/2 tsp ground
- **Oregano** — 1 tsp dried
- **Bay leaves** — 1 leaf
- **Olive oil** — for brushing eggplant and the sofrito
- **Butter** — for the béchamel, about 5 tbsp
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1/2 cup for the béchamel roux
- **Milk** — about 3 cups whole milk, warmed
- **Eggs** — yolks (or whole eggs), tempered into the béchamel
- **Nutmeg** — pinch, freshly grated, for the béchamel
- **Parmesan** — grated; use kefalotyri or pecorino if you have it
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 1 tsp

**Steps**

1. Salt the eggplant: lay the 1/2-inch rounds on a rack or towel, sprinkle generously with salt, and rest 30 minutes to draw out bitterness and moisture. Pat very dry.
2. Roast the vegetables: brush eggplant and potato slices with olive oil, season, and roast at 425F (220C) on sheet pans until softened and lightly browned, about 20-25 minutes, flipping once. Set aside and lower oven to 350F (175C).
3. Build the meat sauce: in a wide pan, soften the diced onion in olive oil, then add minced garlic. Push aside and brown the ground beef, breaking it up until well caramelized and any liquid has cooked off.
4. Stir in the tomato paste and toast 1 minute, then deglaze with the red wine and reduce by half. Add crushed tomatoes, cinnamon, allspice, oregano, bay leaf, salt and pepper. Simmer uncovered 20-25 minutes until thick and nearly dry; discard the bay leaf.
5. Make the béchamel: melt the butter, whisk in the flour and cook the roux 2 minutes without browning. Gradually whisk in the warm milk and cook, stirring, until thick enough to coat a spoon. Off heat, season with salt and a pinch of nutmeg, whisk in most of the cheese, then temper in the egg yolks so they thicken without scrambling.
6. Layer the casserole: in an oiled baking dish, lay the potato slices, then the eggplant, then spread the meat sauce evenly. Top with a second layer of eggplant if you have it, then pour the béchamel over and smooth it out. Dust with the remaining cheese.
7. Bake at 350F (175C) for 45-50 minutes until the top is set and deeply golden. If it needs color, broil for the final 2-3 minutes.
8. Rest at least 30 minutes before cutting so the layers firm up into clean squares; it slices best when warm rather than piping hot.

</details>

Greek-style chicken gyros: thighs marinated in yogurt, lemon, garlic and oregano, seared until charred and juicy, then piled into warm pita with cool tzatziki, tomato, red onion and a few fries. Big, garlicky, bright Mediterranean flavor from a humble weeknight setup.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chicken thighs** — boneless skinless thighs
- **Greek yogurt** — split: most for marinade, rest for tzatziki
- **Lemon** — 1 lemon
- **Garlic** — cloves, divided between marinade and tzatziki
- **Olive oil** — for marinade and pan
- **Oregano** — dried Greek oregano
- **Paprika** — 0.25 tsp
- **Cumin** — 0.2 tsp
- **Salt** — 0.1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.1 tsp
- **Pita bread** — thick Greek-style pita
- **Cucumber** — for tzatziki
- **Fresh dill** — or mint, for tzatziki
- **Tomato** — sliced, to fill
- **Red onion** — thinly sliced
- **Frozen french fries** — traditional gyro filling _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make the marinade: in a bowl whisk about two-thirds of the Greek yogurt with olive oil, the juice of half the lemon, 3 grated garlic cloves, the oregano, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper. Add the chicken thighs and turn to coat, then marinate at least 30 minutes (overnight is better).

3. If using fries, bake or air-fry them per package directions so they are ready when the chicken is done.
4. Heat a heavy skillet or grill pan over medium-high with a thin film of oil. Scrape excess marinade off the thighs and lay them in the hot pan in a single layer.
5. Sear undisturbed 5 to 6 minutes until deeply browned and charred at the edges, then flip and cook another 4 to 5 minutes until the chicken reaches 165F and is cooked through.
6. Rest the chicken a few minutes, then slice into thin strips on a diagonal to mimic shaved gyro meat.
7. Warm the pitas: brush lightly with oil and toast 30 to 60 seconds per side in the hot pan until soft and puffed.
8. Build each gyro: smear tzatziki down the center of a warm pita, add sliced chicken, tomato, red onion and a few fries, top with more tzatziki, then fold and serve.

#### 🥭 [Mango Sago](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-mango-sago/)

The chilled Hong Kong–style dessert: ripe mango blended into a silky coconut-and-evaporated-milk base, studded with bouncy tapioca sago pearls and cubes of fresh mango. Cold, creamy, fruity, and barely any cooking — just boil the pearls and blend.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Sago pearls (tapioca, dry)** — small tapioca/sago pearls, dry
- **Mango** — very ripe — about 2 blended, 1 diced

6. Ladle into bowls or glasses and top with the reserved diced mango (add a few fresh pomelo segments for the classic mango-pomelo version). Serve cold.

#### 🫛 [Chana Chole](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-chana-chole/)

Punjabi-style chole: tender chickpeas simmered in a deeply spiced onion-tomato-ginger-garlic masala, darkened the traditional way with a black tea bag and finished with garam masala and tangy amchur. Hearty, warming, and entirely plant-based.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chickpeas (canned)** — 2 cans, drained (or ~1.5 cups dried, soaked overnight)
- **Onion** — finely chopped
- **Tomato** — pureed
- **Fresh ginger** — 1 tbsp grated
- **Garlic** — 5-6 cloves, minced
- **Thai chili** — green chilies, slit; sub serrano
- **Vegetable oil** — 3 tbsp
- **Cumin seeds** — 1 tsp, for tempering
- **Bay leaves** — 1 leaf
- **Cinnamon** — 1 small stick
- **Whole cloves** — 3-4 whole
- **Green cardamom** — 2 pods
- **Coriander (ground)** — 1.5 tbsp ground
- **Cumin** — 2 tsp ground
- **Turmeric** — 0.5 tsp
- **Chili powder** — 1.5 tsp Kashmiri-style red chili powder
- **Garam masala** — 1 tsp + a finishing pinch
- **Amchur (dry mango powder)** — 1 tsp dried mango powder for tang
- **Black tea** — 1 tea bag, for traditional dark color _(optional)_
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Fresh cilantro** — chopped, to garnish
- **Lemon** — wedges to serve

**Steps**

1. If using dried chickpeas, soak overnight, then pressure-cook with a little salt and the optional black tea bag (tied in muslin) until very tender; reserve the cooking liquid. 2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom and let them sizzle for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the chopped onions and cook, stirring often, for 8-10 minutes until deeply golden brown - this browning is the backbone of the dish.
4. Stir in the ginger, garlic, and slit green chilies and cook for 1-2 minutes until raw smell disappears.
5. Add the ground coriander, cumin, turmeric, and chili powder; stir for 30 seconds, then pour in the pureed tomatoes. Cook this masala down for 8-10 minutes, until the oil separates and the paste turns thick and glossy.
6. Add the chickpeas and salt, then pour in enough chickpea cooking liquid or water to reach a thick gravy. Lightly mash a spoonful of chickpeas against the side to thicken the sauce.
7. Cover and simmer gently for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, so the chickpeas absorb the masala. Add water if it tightens too much.
8. Stir in the garam masala and amchur powder, simmer 2 more minutes, then taste and adjust salt and tang.
9. Finish with a pinch of garam masala and chopped cilantro. Serve hot with lemon wedges, alongside bhature, rice, or roti.

Crisp, flaky North Indian samosas with a flaky maida pastry shell wrapped around a spiced potato-and-pea filling. The dough is made stiff with a generous rub of oil (moyan) so it fries up bubbly and shatteringly crisp, while the filling is seasoned with toasted cumin, coriander, garam masala, and tangy amchur for that authentic chaat-shop flavor.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — maida / all-purpose flour for the pastry shell
- **Vegetable oil** — split: ~3 tbsp rubbed into dough as moyan, rest for deep-frying
- **Potato** — boiled, peeled and roughly mashed
- **Frozen peas** — 0.4 cup
- **Onion** — finely chopped _(optional)_
- **Fresh ginger** — 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- **Thai chili** — green chilies, finely chopped
- **Cumin seeds** — 1 tsp whole
- **Coriander (ground)** — 1.5 tsp ground coriander
- **Turmeric** — 1/2 tsp
- **Garam masala** — 1 tsp
- **Amchur (dry mango powder)** — 1 tsp dried mango powder for tang
- **Fresh cilantro** — chopped, stirred into filling
- **Salt** — to taste, for dough and filling

**Steps**

1. Make the dough: mix the flour with 1/2 tsp salt and about 3 tbsp of the oil (the moyan), rubbing it in until the mixture holds together when pressed into a fist. Add cold water a little at a time and knead into a stiff, smooth dough. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
2. Make the filling: heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan, crackle the cumin seeds, then add ginger, green chilies, and the optional onion and saute 1-2 minutes. Stir in coriander, turmeric, garam masala, and the peas; cook 2 minutes.
3. Add the boiled, mashed potatoes, amchur, and salt. Mash and fry together 3-4 minutes until well combined and dry, then fold in the chopped cilantro and let cool completely.
4. Divide the rested dough into 4 balls. Roll each into a thin oval about 6 inches long, then cut in half to make two semicircles.
5. Form cones: brush the straight edge with a little water, fold into a cone and press the seam firmly to seal. Fill with about 2 tbsp of potato mixture, then pinch the top opening shut, pleating slightly so it stands upright. Repeat for all 8 samosas.
6. Heat the remaining oil to a moderate 325-330F (165C) -- not screaming hot. Gently slide in the samosas in batches and fry low and slow for 10-12 minutes, turning, until pale golden and blistered with crisp bubbles.
7. Increase heat slightly toward the end to deepen the color to golden brown, then drain on a rack or paper towels.
8. Serve hot with mint-cilantro chutney and tamarind chutney.

#### 🌯 [Sicilian Cannoli](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-sicilian-cannoli/)
Italian · snack · hard  
**$0.31/serving** · 40 kcal · P1 · C4 · F2 · ⏱ 90 min · 🍽 8  

The real deal: crisp, blistered fried shells wrapped around a velvety sweetened ricotta cream studded with dark chocolate, ends dipped in chopped pistachios. The dough is enriched with Marsala for that classic Sicilian snap, and the ricotta is drained hard so the filling stays thick — never runny.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — 00 or all-purpose flour for the shells
- **White sugar** — 1 tbsp for the dough
- **Butter** — 2 tbsp cold, cut in (traditionally lard)
- **Marsala wine** — ~1/3 cup dry Marsala to bind the dough
- **Eggs** — 1 yolk in dough + white to seal shells
- **White wine vinegar** — 1 tsp, tenderizes for blistered bubbles
- **Cocoa powder** — 1 tsp unsweetened, for traditional dark shell tint
- **Vegetable oil** — for deep frying, mostly reusable
- **Ricotta cheese** — whole-milk, drained overnight; sheep's milk if available
- **Powdered sugar** — ~1 cup, plus extra for dusting
- **Vanilla extract** — 0.1 tsp
- **Cinnamon** — 0.02 tsp _(optional)_
- **Dark chocolate chips** — mini chips, half folded in, half for ends
- **Pistachios** — chopped, for dipping the ends
- **Orange** — zest for the filling (or candied orange peel) _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Drain the ricotta: line a sieve with cheesecloth or paper towels, add the ricotta, and refrigerate at least 4 hours (ideally overnight), pressing occasionally to squeeze out moisture — this is the single most important step for a thick filling.
2. Make the dough: whisk flour, sugar, and cocoa, then cut in the cold butter until sandy. Add the egg yolk, vinegar, and enough Marsala to form a stiff dough. Knead 5 minutes until smooth and elastic, wrap, and rest 1 hour at room temperature.
3. Roll the dough paper-thin (about 1/16 inch) with a pasta roller or pin. Cut into 4-inch circles, then stretch each slightly into an oval. Wrap around metal cannoli tubes, brushing the overlap with egg white and pressing firmly to seal.
4. Heat the oil to 360-370F (185C) in a deep pot. Fry the shells, 2-3 at a time, until deep golden and blistered with bubbles, about 1-2 minutes, turning once. Drain on a rack and let cool before sliding off the tubes.
5. Make the filling: beat the drained ricotta smooth with the powdered sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and orange zest until creamy. Fold in half of the chocolate chips. Chill while the shells cool — the filling should be thick enough to hold a peak.
6. Spoon the filling into a piping bag and fill each shell from both ends just before serving, so the shells stay crisp and never go soggy.
7. Press the remaining chocolate chips into one exposed end of each cannolo and the chopped pistachios into the other. Dust with powdered sugar and serve immediately.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

**Steps**

Juicy Japanese pan-fried pork dumplings with a crackling-crisp bottom and tender steamed top, packed with napa cabbage, ginger, and garlic. The classic yaki-gyoza method gives you that signature golden crust and soft chewy wrapper. Served with a punchy soy-vinegar-chili dipping sauce.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Ground pork** — fattier pork (about 20% fat) stays juicy
- **Napa cabbage** — 0.4 piece
- **Scallion** — 0.5 stalk
- **Garlic** — about 3 cloves, finely grated
- **Fresh ginger** — about 1 tbsp finely grated
- **Soy sauce** — 0.06 tbsp
- **Sesame oil** — 0.04 tsp
- **Sake (cooking)** — or dry sherry; helps tenderize
- **Sugar** — 0.01 tbsp
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **White pepper** — 0.01 tsp
- **Dumpling wrappers** — round gyoza skins, about 32-36 wrappers
- **Vegetable oil** — for pan-frying
- **Cornstarch** — 1 tsp dissolved in water makes the lacy crispy 'wing' _(optional)_
- **Rice vinegar** — for dipping sauce
- **Chili oil** — rayu, for dipping sauce _(optional)_

**Steps**

5. Pour in about 1/3 cup water (mix in the optional cornstarch first for a crispy lace skirt), cover immediately, and steam for 5-6 minutes until the water evaporates and the wrappers turn translucent.
6. Uncover, drizzle a little more oil around the edges, and let the bottoms re-crisp for 1-2 minutes until shatteringly crisp.
7. Invert onto a plate so the golden bottoms face up. Whisk together rice vinegar, a splash of soy sauce, and chili oil for dipping. Serve hot.

- **Sirloin steak** — thinly sliced against the grain for bulgogi

8. Top each bowl with a fried egg, add a spoonful of gochujang sauce in the center, and garnish with sliced scallion greens and toasted sesame seeds.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Beef sirloin** — ribeye or sirloin, freezer-firmed and sliced paper-thin against the grain
- **Pear** — Korean or Asian pear, peeled and grated (natural tenderizer)
- **Low-sodium soy sauce** — tablespoons soy sauce
- **Brown sugar** — tablespoons (or palm sugar)
- **Honey** — tablespoon, for gloss and balance
- **Toasted sesame oil** — tablespoon
- **Garlic** — 5 cloves, grated or minced
- **Fresh ginger** — 1-inch knob, grated
- **Scallion** — 3-4 stalks, cut into 2-inch lengths
- **White onion** — thinly sliced
- **Black pepper** — freshly ground

6. Let each batch sear undisturbed for about 60-90 seconds to caramelize, then toss and cook another minute until the edges are browned and the marinade glazes the meat.
7. Transfer each finished batch to a plate and repeat with the rest, scraping up any caramelized bits between rounds.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Glass noodles / cellophane noodles** — dangmyeon (sweet potato starch noodles), ~200g dry
- **Beef sirloin** — cut into thin strips
- **Spinach** — half a bunch, blanched
- **Carrot** — julienned
- **Onion** — thinly sliced
- **Shiitake mushrooms** — stems removed, thinly sliced
- **Scallion** — cut into 2-inch lengths

- **Soy sauce** — tablespoons total, split for sauce + beef marinade
- **Sugar** — tablespoons total, split
- **Toasted sesame oil** — tablespoons total, split
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Sesame seeds** — tablespoon, toasted, for garnish
- **Vegetable oil** — for stir-frying
- **Black pepper** — pinch for beef
- **Salt** — to season vegetables

**Steps**

1. Marinate the beef: toss sirloin strips with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, a third of the minced garlic, and a pinch of black pepper. Set aside 15 minutes. Slice shiitake and toss with a splash of soy sauce and sesame oil.

5. In the same hot pan, stir-fry the marinated beef until just cooked through and no longer pink, 2-3 minutes; add it to the bowl.
6. Add the drained noodles and the master sauce to the pan over medium heat. Toss for 1-2 minutes so the noodles absorb the sauce and turn glossy and slightly darkened.
7. Return all the cooked vegetables, beef, and spinach to the pan (or combine everything in the large bowl). Toss thoroughly with hands or tongs so every strand is evenly coated and seasoned; taste and adjust with a little more soy or sugar.
8. Finish with toasted sesame seeds and a final drizzle of sesame oil. Top with thin egg ribbons if using, and serve warm or at room temperature.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

- **Coconut milk** — full-fat, ~1.5 cans
- **Chicken broth** — about 4 cups / 1 L
- **Chicken thighs** — boneless skinless, 1 per serving
- **Rice vermicelli** — dried, soaked/cooked; ~1 portion per serving
- **Tofu puffs** — fried tofu puffs, halved
- **Bean sprouts** — blanched, for topping
- **Eggs** — soft-boiled, halved, 1/2 per serving
- **Shrimp paste** — belacan, ~1 tsp to deepen the broth _(optional)_
- **Fish sauce** — to season, ~1 tbsp
- **Sugar** — palm or white sugar, ~2 tsp
- **Vegetable oil** — for frying the paste, ~2 tbsp

7. Divide the vermicelli among 4 bowls and top with sliced chicken, tofu puffs, and bean sprouts. Ladle the hot laksa broth over everything.

Tinga poblana is a Puebla classic where the deep smoke of chipotles in adobo does all the heavy lifting — easy weeknight cooking, taqueria-level flavor.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chicken thighs** — boneless skinless, for poaching and shredding
- **Tomato** — ripe roma; or sub 1 can crushed tomatoes
- **Chipotle peppers in adobo** — 2-3 chiles plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce, to taste
- **White onion** — half sliced for the sauce, half raw for garnish
- **Garlic** — cloves
- **Chicken broth** — reserve from poaching the chicken
- **Bay leaves** — 1 leaf
- **Oregano** — Mexican oregano preferred, dried
- **Vegetable oil** — tbsp, for frying the sauce
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Tortilla** — corn tortillas, 3 per person
- **Queso fresco** — crumbled, for topping
- **Avocado** — sliced, for topping
- **Fresh cilantro** — chopped, for garnish
- **Lime** — cut into wedges

**Steps**

1. Put the chicken thighs in a saucepan with half the onion (kept in a chunk), 1 garlic clove, the bay leaf, and a big pinch of salt. Cover with water, bring to a gentle simmer, and poach 18-20 minutes until cooked through. Lift out the chicken and reserve about 1/3 cup of the poaching broth.

5. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high. Add the remaining sliced onion and cook until softened and lightly golden, 4-5 minutes.
6. Pour in the blended chipotle-tomato sauce (stand back, it will splatter) and fry it, stirring, for 5 minutes until it darkens and thickens slightly.
7. Add the shredded chicken, toss to coat, and simmer 5-8 minutes until the chicken has soaked up the sauce and it clings without being soupy. Season with salt and add a splash of broth if it tightens too much.
8. Warm the corn tortillas on a dry skillet until pliable and lightly toasted, stacking them under a towel to keep them soft.
9. Build the tacos: a heap of tinga per tortilla, topped with raw onion, crumbled queso fresco, avocado, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Pork shoulder** — bone-in if possible, cut into 2-inch chunks
- **Hominy (canned)** — white hominy, drained and rinsed (about two 25 oz cans)
- **Dried chili** — 6 guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
- **Ancho chili powder** — stand-in for 3 whole ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
- **White onion** — half for the broth, the rest diced for garnish
- **Garlic** — about 6 cloves
- **Oregano** — Mexican oregano if you can find it
- **Cumin** — 1 tsp
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaves
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Cabbage** — thinly shredded, for garnish
- **Radish** — thinly sliced, for garnish
- **Lime** — cut into wedges, for garnish
- **Fresh cilantro** — chopped, for garnish _(optional)_
- **Tortilla chips** — or warm corn tostadas on the side _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Put the pork shoulder, half a white onion (left in one chunk), the garlic cloves, bay leaves, and a big pinch of salt in a large pot. 6. Pour the strained chile sauce into the broth through the sieve and stir. Add the shredded pork and the drained hominy. Crumble in the Mexican oregano, rubbing it between your palms to release its oils.

8. Set out bowls of shredded cabbage, sliced radish, diced onion, chopped cilantro, and lime wedges. Ladle the hot pozole into bowls and let everyone garnish their own, finishing with a generous squeeze of lime. Serve with tortilla chips or tostadas on the side.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, finely minced or grated

#### 🧆 [Crispy Falafel](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-crispy-falafel/)
Middle Eastern · lunch · medium  
**$1.49/serving** · 452 kcal · P16 · C82 · F6 · ⏱ 45 min · 🍽 4  

Shatteringly crisp, herb-flecked falafel made the real way — from soaked dried chickpeas (never canned) blitzed with parsley, cilantro, onion, garlic and warm spices, then fried until deep golden and fluffy inside. Pile into pita with tahini sauce or eat them straight.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chickpeas (canned)** — DRIED chickpeas, soaked 18-24h — NOT canned/cooked (canned falafel falls apart)
- **Onion** — small, roughly chopped
- **Garlic** — cloves
- **Fresh parsley** — large bunch flat-leaf, leaves + tender stems
- **Fresh cilantro** — bunch, leaves + tender stems
- **Cumin** — tsp, ideally ground from toasted seeds
- **Coriander (ground)** — tsp ground
- **Cayenne pepper** — tsp, optional heat
- **Cardamom** — tsp ground, optional warm note
- **Salt** — tsp
- **Black pepper** — tsp
- **All-purpose flour** — tbsp chickpea or AP flour, only if mix is loose
- **Baking soda** — tsp, stir in just before frying for fluffiness
- **Sesame seeds** — tbsp, pressed onto patties for extra crunch _(optional)_
- **Vegetable oil** — for deep frying, ~3-4 cups; reusable
- **Tahini** — for sauce
- **Lemon** — juiced, for tahini sauce

**Steps**

1. The day before: cover the dried chickpeas with plenty of cold water and soak 18-24 hours until fully doubled and split-tender when pressed. Do NOT use canned or cooked chickpeas — they hold too much water and the falafel will disintegrate. Drain very well and pat dry.
2. In a food processor, pulse the onion, garlic, parsley, and cilantro until finely minced but not pureed, scraping down the sides. Add the drained raw chickpeas, cumin, coriander, cayenne, cardamom, salt, and black pepper.
3. Pulse in short bursts until the mixture looks like coarse, damp sand that clumps when squeezed — fine enough to hold together but still slightly textured. Don't over-process into a paste. If it feels wet, pulse in the flour.
4. Transfer to a bowl, cover, and chill at least 1 hour (up to overnight) so it firms up and the flavors bloom.
5. Just before frying, sprinkle the baking soda over the mix and fold it in — this gives a light, fluffy interior. Scoop and press into 1.5-inch balls or slightly flattened patties; if the seams crack, squeeze a bit tighter. Optionally press sesame seeds onto the tops.
6. Heat 1.5 inches of oil in a deep pot to 350F (175C). Whisk the tahini with lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and cold water a tablespoon at a time until it loosens into a pourable, creamy sauce.
7. Fry the falafel in batches, without crowding, for 3-4 minutes, turning once, until deep mahogany-brown and crisp all over. Test one first — if it breaks apart, work in a little more flour; if dense, add a touch more baking soda.
8. Drain on a wire rack or paper towels and season with a pinch of salt while hot. Serve immediately with the lemon-tahini sauce — they're best within minutes of frying.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chicken thighs** — bone-in, skin-on thighs (about 1 per serving)
- **Onion** — grated or finely sliced
- **Garlic** — 4 cloves, minced
- **Fresh ginger** — 1 tbsp fresh grated
- **Saffron threads** — a generous pinch, bloomed in warm water
- **Turmeric** — 1 tsp ground
- **Ground ginger** — 1 tsp ground ginger
- **Cumin** — 1.5 tsp ground
- **Cinnamon** — 1 cinnamon stick or 1/2 tsp ground
- **Paprika** — 1 tsp sweet
- **Olive oil** — 3 tbsp
- **Preserved lemon** — rind only, rinsed and sliced
- **Green olives (jarred)** — about 2/3 cup, cracked green olives
- **Fresh cilantro** — half a bunch, plus parsley if you have it

- **Lemon** — juice of half, to finish

- **Black pepper** — to taste

7. Add the preserved lemon rind and green olives, scattering them around the chicken. Simmer uncovered 10-12 minutes more to meld the flavors and reduce the sauce to a silky consistency.

#### 🫘 [Rajma Masala](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-rajma-masala/)
North Indian · dinner · medium  
**$1.69/serving** · 874 kcal · P25 · C187 · F3 · ⏱ 60 min · 🍽 4  

North Indian comfort food at its finest: red kidney beans simmered until creamy in a slow-cooked onion-tomato masala perfumed with ginger, garlic, and warm whole spices. Finished with garam masala and fresh cilantro, it is rich, soupy, and made to be spooned over fluffy basmati rice (rajma-chawal).

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Kidney beans (canned)** — red kidney beans (rajma); 2 cans drained, or 1.5 cups dried soaked overnight
- **Onion** — finely chopped
- **Tomato** — pureed, or 1 can crushed tomatoes
- **Fresh ginger** — 1 tbsp grated
- **Garlic** — 5-6 cloves, minced
- **Ghee** — 3 tbsp; or vegetable oil for vegan
- **Cumin** — 1 tsp whole cumin seeds for tempering
- **Bay leaves** — 1 leaf
- **Green cardamom** — 2 pods
- **Whole cloves** — 2-3 whole
- **Cinnamon** — 1 small stick
- **Coriander (ground)** — 2 tsp ground coriander
- **Turmeric** — 0.5 tsp
- **Chili powder** — 1.5 tsp Kashmiri chili powder for color
- **Cayenne pepper** — 0.25 tsp, optional for heat _(optional)_
- **Garam masala** — 1 tsp, added at the end
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Fresh cilantro** — chopped, for garnish
- **Basmati rice** — steamed, for serving

**Steps**

1. Drain and rinse the kidney beans (if using dried, soak overnight then pressure-cook with a little salt until fully soft and mashable). Set aside; reserve some bean liquid or water.
2. Heat the ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds, bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon and let them sizzle for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the chopped onion and cook, stirring often, for 8-10 minutes until deep golden brown - this color is the backbone of the dish.
4. Stir in the ginger and garlic and cook 1 minute, then add the ground coriander, turmeric, and chili powder and stir for 20 seconds to bloom the spices.
5. Pour in the tomato puree, add salt, and cook this masala down for 8-10 minutes, stirring, until the oil separates and the mixture is thick and jammy (bhuna).
6. Add the kidney beans along with about 2 cups of water (or reserved bean liquid). Lightly mash a spoonful of beans against the side to thicken the gravy.
7. Bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered for 20-25 minutes until the beans are creamy and the gravy coats a spoon. Add the cayenne now if using.
8. Stir in the garam masala, taste and adjust salt, and simmer 2 more minutes. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot over steamed basmati rice.

</details>

#### 🥩 [Peruvian Lomo Saltado](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-peruvian-lomo-saltado/)
Peruvian · dinner · medium  
**$2.22/serving** · 796 kcal · P15 · C154 · F12 · ⏱ 40 min · 🍽 4  

Peru's iconic stir-fry: strips of seared sirloin tossed with red onion, tomato, and aji amarillo, deglazed with soy sauce and red wine vinegar, then folded together with crispy french fries and served over white rice. A delicious clash of Andean and Chinese-Peruvian (chifa) cooking.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Sirloin steak** — beef sirloin or tenderloin, cut into 1/2-inch strips
- **Red onion** — large, cut into thick wedges/petals
- **Tomato** — firm Roma, seeded and cut into wedges
- **Aji amarillo paste** — Peruvian yellow chili paste
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Soy sauce** — tablespoons
- **Red wine vinegar** — tablespoons
- **Fresh cilantro** — roughly chopped, to finish
- **Frozen french fries** — or hand-cut potatoes deep-fried
- **Jasmine rice** — white rice, steamed, to serve
- **Vegetable oil** — tablespoons, for high-heat searing
- **Salt** — to taste
- **Black pepper** — freshly ground, to taste
- **Cumin** — teaspoon
- **Jalapeño** — aji amarillo strips if you can find them, else a thin chili for heat _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Cook the fries first so they're hot and crisp at the end: bake or air-fry the frozen fries (or deep-fry hand-cut potatoes) until golden; hold warm. Steam the jasmine rice separately.
2. Pat the sirloin strips very dry and season with salt, pepper, and the cumin. Drying is key so the beef sears instead of steaming.
3. Get a large skillet or wok screaming hot with 1 tablespoon oil. Working in 2 batches so you never crowd the pan, sear the beef hard for about 1 minute per side until browned but still pink inside; remove to a plate. Add a little oil between batches.
4. In the same hot pan add another tablespoon of oil, then the red onion wedges. Stir-fry 1 to 2 minutes so they char at the edges but stay crisp-tender, not soft.
5. Add the garlic and aji amarillo paste and toss for 30 seconds until fragrant, then add the tomato wedges and stir-fry just 1 minute so they soften but hold their shape.
6. Return the beef and its juices to the pan, then deglaze by pouring the soy sauce and red wine vinegar down the hot sides of the pan; toss hard for 30 to 60 seconds, scraping up the browned bits into a glossy pan sauce. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
7. Kill the heat and gently fold the hot fries through the saltado so they soak up the sauce but stay crisp; add the optional chili strips here.
8. Scatter the chopped cilantro over the top and serve immediately, with the steamed white rice on the side.

</details>

#### 🥟 [Polish Pierogi (Pierogi Ruskie)](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-polish-pierogi/)
Polish · dinner · medium  
**$0.95/serving** · 251 kcal · P9 · C47 · F3 · ⏱ 90 min · 🍽 4  

Tender hand-pinched dumplings stuffed with creamy potato and farmer's cheese, boiled then pan-fried in butter with golden onions. The classic Polish pierogi ruskie — comfort food worth the folding.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose, about 2.5 cups for the dough
- **Eggs** — binds the dough
- **Sour cream** — 2 tbsp in dough, rest to serve
- **Potato** — starchy, for the filling
- **Cottage cheese** — stand-in for Polish twaróg/farmer's cheese; drain well
- **Onion** — one for filling, one for frying
- **Butter** — for sauteing onions and finishing
- **Salt** — 0.05 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.03 tsp

**Steps**

1. Make the dough: mound the flour, make a well, and add the egg, 2 tbsp sour cream, a big pinch of salt, and about 3/4 cup warm water. Mix, then knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest 30 minutes while you make the filling.
2. Boil the peeled, chunked potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 15 minutes; drain well and mash until smooth with no lumps.
3. Finely dice one onion and saute in 1 tbsp butter until soft and golden; stir into the warm mashed potato along with the drained cottage cheese, then season generously with salt and pepper. Let the filling cool.
4. Roll the rested dough thin (about 2mm) on a floured surface and cut out 3-inch rounds with a glass or cutter, re-rolling scraps.
5. Place a teaspoon of filling on each round, fold into a half-moon, and pinch the edges firmly to seal — make sure no filling reaches the seam or they'll burst.
6. Bring a wide pot of salted water to a gentle boil and cook the pierogi in batches; they're done about 2-3 minutes after they float to the surface. Lift out with a slotted spoon.
7. Meanwhile, slice the second onion and fry slowly in the remaining butter until deeply golden and sweet.
8. Add the drained boiled pierogi to the buttery onion pan and fry 1-2 minutes per side until lightly crisp and golden.
9. Serve hot, topped with the fried onions and butter and a dollop of sour cream.

</details>

#### 🌽 [Skillet Cornbread](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-skillet-cornbread/)
Southern American · snack · easy  
**$0.15/serving** · 32 kcal · P2 · C2 · F2 · ⏱ 40 min · 🍽 8  

Southern-style cast-iron cornbread with a crackly, butter-fried crust and a tender, golden crumb. Cornmeal-forward and barely sweet, baked in a screaming-hot skillet so the edges crisp up while the center stays moist. Best torn warm and slathered with butter.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Cornmeal (polenta)** — 1 1/2 cups stone-ground yellow cornmeal, medium grind
- **All-purpose flour** — 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- **Baking powder** — 1 tablespoon
- **Baking soda** — 1/2 teaspoon
- **Salt** — 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- **Buttermilk** — 1 1/2 cups, full-fat
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Butter** — 5 tablespoons; 1 tbsp melts in the hot skillet, 4 in the batter
- **Honey** — 2 tablespoons; omit for a fully savory Southern loaf _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Place a 10-inch cast-iron skillet on the middle rack and preheat the oven to 425F (220C) with the skillet inside for at least 15 minutes so it gets ripping hot.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt until evenly combined.
3. In a second bowl, whisk the eggs, buttermilk, honey (if using), and 4 tablespoons melted butter until smooth.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir with a spatula just until no dry streaks remain; the batter should be thick but pourable. Do not overmix, or the crumb turns tough.
5. Pull the hot skillet out, add the remaining 1 tablespoon butter, and swirl so it foams and coats the bottom and sides; it should sizzle on contact.
6. Immediately pour the batter into the skillet, listening for that sizzle that sets the crust, and smooth the top.
7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the top is deep golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Let rest in the skillet for 5 minutes, then run a knife around the edge, invert onto a board or cut into wedges, and serve warm with butter.

</details>

#### 🥬 [Southern Collard Greens](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-southern-collard-greens/)
Southern American · dinner · easy  
**$0.52/serving** · 13 kcal · P0 · C2 · F0 · ⏱ 150 min · 🍽 6  

Slow-simmered collard greens braised low and slow with a smoky ham hock, sweet onion, and garlic until silky and tender. Finished with a splash of apple cider vinegar and a hit of hot sauce, with that prized savory broth — the "pot likker" — soaking up every bit of flavor. Pure Southern soul food comfort.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Collard greens** — 2 large bunches, stems removed, leaves chopped
- **Sliced ham** — 1 meaty smoked ham hock (or 1 smoked turkey leg)
- **Onion** — 1 large yellow onion, diced
- **Garlic** — 4 cloves, minced
- **Chicken broth** — about 4 cups low-sodium
- **Apple cider vinegar** — about 2 tbsp, plus more to taste
- **Sugar** — 1 tsp, balances bitterness
- **Red pepper flakes** — 1/2 tsp, plus more to taste
- **Hot sauce** — to taste at the table _(optional)_
- **Olive oil** — 1 tbsp for sweating the onion
- **Salt** — 0.05 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.05 tsp

**Steps**

1. Strip the collard leaves from their tough center stems, then stack, roll, and slice into wide ribbons. Wash thoroughly in cold water two or three times to remove all grit, and drain.
2. In a large heavy pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook 1 minute more until fragrant.
3. Nestle the smoked ham hock into the pot and pour in the chicken broth plus enough water to nearly cover the hock. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer, cover, and cook 45 minutes to draw out the smoky flavor and start tenderizing the meat.
4. Add the chopped collards to the pot in batches, stirring and letting each addition wilt down before adding the next until all the greens fit and are submerged in the simmering pot likker.
5. Stir in the sugar, then cover and simmer gently over low heat for 1 to 1.5 hours, stirring occasionally, until the greens are meltingly tender and dark.
6. Remove the ham hock, pull the meat from the bone, shred it, and stir it back into the greens; discard the bone, skin, and fat.
7. Stir in the apple cider vinegar, then taste and season with salt and pepper, adding more vinegar or red pepper flakes to balance.
8. Serve hot with plenty of the pot likker spooned over, passing hot sauce at the table.

</details>

#### 🥨 [Cinnamon Churros](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-cinnamon-churros/)
Spanish · snack · medium  
**$0.26/serving** · 86 kcal · P4 · C8 · F4 · ⏱ 35 min · 🍽 4  

Golden, ridged churros with a shatteringly crisp shell and tender, custardy center, fried fresh and rolled in warm cinnamon sugar. The classic Spanish-Mexican fairground treat, easy to nail at home with a piped choux dough and a hot pot of oil.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose, about 1 cup
- **Water** — 1 cup, for the dough
- **Butter** — 3 tbsp, cut into pieces
- **Sugar** — 1 tbsp in dough + ~1/2 cup for coating
- **Salt** — 1/4 tsp
- **Eggs** — lightly beaten, for a richer choux dough
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Cinnamon** — 1 to 1.5 tbsp for the coating
- **Vegetable oil** — for deep frying, about 4 cups
- **Dulce de leche** — or chocolate sauce, for dipping _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make the cinnamon sugar: stir together about 1/2 cup sugar with the cinnamon in a wide shallow dish and set aside. Fit a piping bag with a large closed-star tip.
2. In a saucepan, combine the water, butter, the 1 tbsp sugar, and salt. Bring to a rolling boil over medium-high heat so the butter fully melts.
3. Reduce heat to medium, dump in all the flour at once, and stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until it forms a smooth ball that pulls away from the pan, about 1 minute. Remove from heat and let cool 5 minutes.
4. Beat in the vanilla, then add the eggs a little at a time, stirring hard after each addition until the dough is glossy, thick, and smooth. Transfer to the piping bag.
5. Heat 2 to 3 inches of oil in a deep pot to 350-360F (175C). Pipe 5 to 6 inch lengths of dough directly into the oil, snipping the end with scissors, and fry 3 to 4 at a time so the oil stays hot.
6. Fry, turning occasionally, until deep golden brown and crisp all over, about 2 to 3 minutes per batch. Don't crowd the pot or they turn greasy.
7. Lift out with tongs or a spider and drain on a paper-towel-lined rack for 30 seconds.
8. While still hot, roll each churro in the cinnamon sugar to coat. Serve immediately with dulce de leche or warm chocolate for dipping.

</details>

#### 🍝 [Swedish Meatballs (Köttbullar)](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-swedish-meatballs/)
Swedish · dinner · medium  
**$1.59/serving** · 372 kcal · P18 · C45 · F14 · ⏱ 50 min · 🍽 4  

Tender beef-and-pork meatballs bound with a milk-soaked breadcrumb panade and warmed with allspice and nutmeg, pan-fried and simmered in a silky cream gravy. Served the classic way with a spoonful of lingonberry jam.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Ground beef** — half of the meat mix
- **Ground pork** — other half of the meat mix
- **Italian breadcrumbs** — for the milk panade
- **Milk** — to soak the breadcrumbs
- **Onion** — finely grated or minced, sweated in butter
- **Eggs** — 1 egg
- **Allspice** — ground; the defining warm spice
- **Nutmeg** — 0.25 tsp
- **Salt** — 0.2 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.15 tsp
- **Butter** — for frying meatballs and the gravy roux
- **All-purpose flour** — for the gravy roux
- **Beef broth** — about 2 cups
- **Heavy cream** — for the cream gravy
- **Soy sauce** — small splash for color and savory depth
- **Dijon mustard** — stirred into the gravy _(optional)_
- **Jam** — lingonberry jam, to serve on the side
- **Potato** — boiled or mashed, to serve _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Tear or measure the breadcrumbs into a bowl, pour over the milk, and let soak for 5-10 minutes until the mixture is soft and pasty.
2. Melt a small knob of the butter in a skillet over medium heat and gently sweat the finely grated onion for 3-4 minutes until soft and translucent but not browned; let it cool slightly.
3. In a large bowl combine the ground beef, ground pork, soaked breadcrumb panade, cooked onion, egg, allspice, nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands just until uniform, then roll into small balls about 1 inch across (wet hands help).
4. Melt the remaining butter in the skillet over medium heat and fry the meatballs in batches, rolling them around so they brown on all sides and are nearly cooked through, about 6-8 minutes per batch. Transfer to a plate.
5. In the same pan with all the browned bits, sprinkle in the flour and whisk into the butter and fat to form a roux; cook 1 minute until lightly golden.
6. Gradually whisk in the beef broth, then the cream, scraping up the fond. Add the soy sauce and Dijon and simmer, whisking, until the gravy thickens enough to coat a spoon, 3-5 minutes.
7. Return the meatballs and any juices to the pan, lower the heat, and simmer gently for 5-8 minutes until they are cooked through and coated in the silky gravy. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
8. Serve hot over boiled or mashed potatoes with a spoonful of lingonberry jam on the side.

</details>

#### 🌮 [Breakfast Tacos](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-breakfast-tacos/)
Tex-Mex · breakfast · easy  
**$3.37/serving** · 740 kcal · P29 · C75 · F37 · ⏱ 25 min · 🍽 2  

Authentic Tex-Mex breakfast tacos: soft, slow-scrambled eggs folded with crispy bacon and melty cheddar, tucked into warm corn tortillas and finished with bright pico de gallo, avocado, and a squeeze of lime. The kind they sell out of by 9am in Austin.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Eggs** — 4 large eggs
- **Tortilla** — 6 small corn tortillas (or flour)
- **Bacon** — 4 strips, about 2 servings
- **Sharp cheddar block** — about 2/3 cup shredded sharp cheddar
- **Butter** — 1 tbsp for the eggs
- **Avocado** — 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- **Pico de gallo** — about 1/2 cup fresh pico de gallo
- **Fresh cilantro** — small handful, chopped
- **Lime** — wedges for serving
- **Hot sauce** — a few dashes to taste _(optional)_
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.02 tsp

**Steps**

1. Lay the bacon in a cold skillet, set over medium heat, and cook 6-8 minutes, flipping once, until crisp. Transfer to a paper towel and pour off all but a thin film of fat (save the rendered fat).
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper and whisk well until uniform and slightly frothy. Roughly chop the cooked bacon.
3. Warm the tortillas: set a dry skillet or comal over medium-high and toast each tortilla 20-30 seconds per side until soft and lightly charred in spots. Stack and wrap in a clean towel to keep warm and pliable.
4. Wipe the skillet, lower the heat to medium-low, and melt the butter. Pour in the eggs and let them sit 10 seconds, then push gently with a spatula, folding from the edges to the center to form large soft curds.
5. When the eggs are still glossy and a touch underdone, stir in the chopped bacon and the shredded cheddar so it melts into the curds. Pull off the heat immediately while soft.
6. Build each taco: spread a few curds of the bacon-and-cheese egg down the center of a warm tortilla, top with sliced avocado, a spoon of pico de gallo, and a scatter of cilantro.
7. Finish with a squeeze of lime and a dash of hot sauce, and serve right away while the tortillas are warm.

</details>

#### 🍜 [Chicken Pad Thai](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur-chicken-pad-thai/)
Thai · dinner · medium  
**$3.64/serving** · 472 kcal · P46 · C51 · F10 · ⏱ 45 min · 🍽 2  

The real-deal Bangkok street-cart noodle stir-fry: chewy rice noodles tossed in a sweet-sour-savory tamarind sauce with chicken, scrambled egg, crisp bean sprouts, and a finish of crushed peanuts and lime. The secret is a screaming-hot wok, a proper tamarind-fish sauce-palm sugar balance, and tossing fast so the noodles never turn to mush.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Rice noodles** — flat 5mm pad thai noodles, soaked in warm water until pliable
- **Chicken breast** — sliced thin against the grain
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Tamarind paste** — tamarind concentrate/paste, about 2 tbsp
- **Fish sauce** — about 2.5 tbsp
- **Palm sugar** — about 2 tbsp, brown sugar works too
- **Bean sprouts** — half raw, half tossed at the end
- **Scallion** — cut into 2-inch batons
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Shallot** — thinly sliced
- **Peanuts** — roasted, coarsely crushed
- **Lime** — cut into wedges for serving
- **Vegetable oil** — for the wok
- **Dried shrimp** — chopped, for traditional umami _(optional)_
- **Red pepper flakes** — Thai chili flakes for serving _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Soak the rice noodles in warm (not boiling) water for 20-30 minutes until pliable but still firm with a slight bite, then drain well. They will finish cooking in the wok, so do not let them go fully soft.
2. Make the sauce: whisk the tamarind paste, fish sauce, and palm sugar together until the sugar dissolves. Taste and adjust until it is balanced sweet, sour, and salty all at once.
3. Heat a wok or large skillet over the highest heat until smoking, add the oil, then sear the sliced chicken until just cooked through and lightly browned. Push it to one side.
4. Add the garlic, shallot, and dried shrimp (if using) to the empty side and stir-fry 20-30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add the drained noodles and pour in the sauce. Toss constantly so the noodles absorb the sauce and soften, adding a splash of water if they look dry, until they are tender and chewy.
6. Push everything aside, crack in the eggs, scramble them lightly until just set, then fold them through the noodles.
7. Turn off the heat and toss in half the bean sprouts, the scallion batons, and half the crushed peanuts so the sprouts stay crisp.
8. Plate immediately and top with the remaining raw bean sprouts, crushed peanuts, Thai chili flakes, and a lime wedge to squeeze over at the table.

</details>
