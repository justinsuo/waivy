#### 🥦 [Orecchiette with Broccoli Rabe](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-orecchiette-with-broccoli-rabe/)
Italian · dinner · easy  
**$1.15/serving** · 206 kcal · P8 · C43 · F2 · ⏱ 30 min · 🍽 4  

The classic Puglian one-pot pasta: ear-shaped orecchiette boiled together with bitter broccoli rabe, then tossed in a garlicky, anchovy-melted, chili-spiked olive oil. Earthy, savory, and just a little bitter, finished with sharp pecorino. Rustic Southern Italian comfort food.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Pasta** — orecchiette (ear-shaped pasta), ~12-14 oz dry
- **Broccolini** — broccoli rabe (rapini); broccolini is a milder, sweeter stand-in — 1 large bunch, trimmed
- **Garlic** — 4 fat cloves, thinly sliced
- **Anchovies in olive oil (canned)** — 4-5 oil-packed fillets; melt into the oil for savory depth
- **Red pepper flakes** — 1/2 tsp, or to taste
- **Olive oil** — 1/4 cup good extra-virgin
- **Pecorino Romano** — grated, for finishing _(optional)_
- **Salt** — for heavily salting the pasta water
- **Lemon** — a squeeze to brighten the bitterness _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and salt it generously until it tastes like the sea. Meanwhile, trim the tough bottom inch off the broccoli rabe stems and cut the rest into 2-inch pieces, keeping leaves, stems, and florets.
2. Drop the broccoli rabe into the boiling water and blanch for 2 minutes to tame its bitterness, then scoop it out with a spider or slotted spoon into a colander, leaving the pot of water on the heat.
3. Add the orecchiette to the same boiling water and cook until al dente, following the package time (usually 10-12 minutes). Reserve a cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
4. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic, anchovy fillets, and red pepper flakes; cook gently for 2-3 minutes, mashing the anchovies with a spoon until they dissolve into the oil and the garlic is golden, not browned.
5. Add the blanched broccoli rabe to the skillet, season lightly with salt, and saute 3-4 minutes so it soaks up the garlicky, anchovy-rich oil.
6. Drain the orecchiette and add it straight to the skillet with a splash of the reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat for a minute so the starchy water emulsifies into a light, glossy sauce that clings to the pasta.
7. Taste and adjust salt and chili. Finish with a squeeze of lemon if using, then plate and shower with grated pecorino. Serve immediately while hot.

</details>

#### 🍝 [Pasta Puttanesca](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-pasta-puttanesca/)
Italian · dinner · easy  
**$2.24/serving** · 48 kcal · P2 · C9 · F1 · ⏱ 30 min · 🍽 4  

The bold, briny Neapolitan pasta that comes together from the pantry in one pan: spaghetti tossed in a garlicky tomato sauce loud with anchovy, capers, and Kalamata olives, brightened with parsley and a kick of chile. No cream, no fuss — just deeply savory, salty-sweet, and ready in under 30 minutes.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Spaghetti** — about 360g dried spaghetti
- **Crushed tomatoes** — one 28oz can, San Marzano if possible
- **Anchovies in olive oil (canned)** — 6-8 oil-packed fillets, finely chopped — they melt into the sauce
- **Capers (jarred)** — 3 tbsp, rinsed and drained
- **Kalamata olives (jarred)** — about 2/3 cup, pitted and torn
- **Garlic** — 4 cloves, thinly sliced
- **Olive oil** — 1/4 cup extra-virgin
- **Red pepper flakes** — 1/2 tsp, or to taste
- **Fresh parsley** — 1/4 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
- **Tomato paste** — 1 tbsp, for depth _(optional)_
- **Salt** — for the pasta water — go light, the sauce is salty
- **Black pepper** — freshly ground, to finish

**Steps**

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it lightly — the anchovies, capers, and olives bring plenty of salt, so don't over-season the water.
2. While it heats, warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes and cook gently for about 1 minute, until fragrant but not browned.
3. Add the chopped anchovies (and tomato paste, if using) and stir for 1-2 minutes, mashing with a wooden spoon until the anchovies dissolve completely into the oil.
4. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, then stir in the olives and capers. Simmer over medium heat for 12-15 minutes, until the sauce thickens and the raw tomato edge mellows.
5. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente, about 1 minute less than the package says. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
6. Transfer the drained spaghetti directly into the skillet with the sauce, adding a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat for 1-2 minutes so the pasta finishes cooking in the sauce and the starch emulsifies it into a glossy coat.
7. Stir in most of the chopped parsley and a few grinds of black pepper. Loosen with more pasta water if needed. Taste and adjust — it should be bold and briny, rarely needing extra salt.
8. Plate immediately, scatter with the remaining parsley, and finish with a drizzle of olive oil. Serve hot; puttanesca is best the moment it's tossed.

</details>

#### 🍄 [Rice Cooker Mushroom Risotto](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/rice-cooker-mushroom-risotto/)
Italian · dinner · easy  
**$2.55/serving** · 105 kcal · P5 · C10 · F6 · ⏱ 45 min · 🍽 4  

Creamy, no-stir parmesan-mushroom risotto that the rice cooker babysits for you while you do literally anything else.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Arborio rice** — arborio (short-grain) rice — gives the creamy texture
- **Mushrooms** — cremini or button mushrooms, sliced
- **Onion** — small yellow onion, finely diced
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Butter** — tablespoons, divided
- **Vegetable broth** — cups, warm
- **White wine (cooking)** — cup dry white wine (optional but classic) _(optional)_
- **Parmesan** — cup finely grated, plus more to finish
- **Fresh thyme** — teaspoon leaves, or 0.5 tsp dried
- **Fresh parsley** — tablespoons chopped, to finish _(optional)_
- **Salt** — teaspoon, to taste
- **Black pepper** — teaspoon black pepper

**Steps**

1. Turn the rice cooker to the Cook/White Rice setting (lid open) and melt 1 tbsp butter in the bowl. Add the diced onion and sliced mushrooms and let them soften and release their liquid, stirring once or twice, about 5-6 minutes.
2. Stir in the minced garlic and thyme for 30 seconds, then add the dry arborio rice and stir to coat it in the butter for about a minute so the grains turn slightly translucent at the edges.
3. Pour in the white wine (if using) and let it bubble off for a minute, then add the warm veggie broth and the salt. Stir once to settle everything evenly.
4. Close the lid and run a full Cook cycle. Most rice cookers will click to Warm before the risotto is done — when it does, give it a good stir, run a SECOND short Cook cycle, and check that the rice is creamy and just tender with a little bite (add a splash more broth or hot water if it looks dry).
5. Switch to Warm, stir in the grated parmesan and the remaining 1 tbsp butter until glossy and creamy. Season with black pepper and more salt to taste.
6. Let it rest with the lid closed for 3-4 minutes to thicken, then stir, top with extra parmesan and chopped parsley, and serve right away.

</details>

#### 🥣 [Rice Cooker Tuscan White Bean & Kale Soup](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/rice-cooker-tuscan-bean-soup/)
Italian · dinner · easy  
**$1.84/serving** · 233 kcal · P14 · C43 · F2 · ⏱ 50 min · 🍽 4  

A cozy, brothy bowl of creamy white beans, tender kale, and sweet tomato that simmers itself in the rice cooker while you do nothing.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Cannellini beans (canned)** — white beans, drained and rinsed
- **Kale** — stems removed, leaves torn
- **Crushed tomatoes** — one 14 oz can
- **Onion** — diced
- **Carrot** — diced
- **Celery** — 2 stalks, diced
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Vegetable broth** — about 4 cups
- **Olive oil** — 1 tbsp
- **Italian seasoning** — 1 tsp
- **Parmesan** — grated, to finish _(optional)_
- **Salt** — 0.1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.1 tsp

**Steps**

1. Add the olive oil, diced onion, carrot, celery, and garlic to the rice cooker pot. Stir, close the lid, and run one cook cycle (about 10 minutes), stirring once, to soften the vegetables.
2. Add the crushed tomatoes, drained white beans, italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Pour in the veggie broth and stir well.
3. Close the lid and run a second full cook cycle so the soup comes up to a steady simmer, about 25-30 minutes. If your cooker clicks off early, just press cook again to keep it bubbling.
4. Open the lid, stir in the torn kale, and let it wilt in the hot soup for 5 minutes (close the lid and run cook again briefly if it needs more heat).
5. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. For a creamier body, mash a few beans against the side of the pot with a spoon.
6. Ladle into bowls and finish with grated parmesan.

</details>

#### 🍝 [Spicy Vodka Pasta (Gigi Hadid)](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/spicy-vodka-pasta/)
Italian · dinner · easy  
**$1.21/serving** · 99 kcal · P1 · C3 · F10 · ⏱ 25 min · 🍽 4  

Gigi Hadid's famous creamy, spicy tomato vodka penne that's silky, cozy, and ready in under half an hour.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Penne** — penne or rigatoni, in ounces
- **Olive oil** — tablespoons
- **Shallot** — or 1/4 onion, finely minced
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Tomato paste** — about a quarter of a 6 oz can
- **Vodka** — tablespoon
- **Heavy cream** — cup
- **Red pepper flakes** — teaspoon, adjust to taste
- **Butter** — tablespoon
- **Parmesan** — cup, grated
- **Fresh basil** — leaves, torn, for garnish _(optional)_
- **Salt** — to taste

**Steps**

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the penne until just shy of al dente. Reserve about 1 cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Add the minced shallot and garlic and saute 2-3 minutes until soft and fragrant but not browned.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring constantly, for 4-5 minutes until the paste deepens to a brick-red color and smells sweet.
4. Pour in the vodka and let it sizzle for about 1 minute to cook off the raw alcohol, then stir in the heavy cream until the sauce turns a smooth pinkish-orange.
5. Add the drained pasta along with a splash of reserved pasta water, the butter, and the parmesan. Toss vigorously over low heat until the sauce clings and turns glossy, adding more pasta water as needed.
6. Season with salt to taste, plate, and finish with extra parmesan and torn fresh basil.

</details>

#### 🍲 [Italian Wedding Soup](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-italian-wedding-soup/)
Italian-American · dinner · medium  
**$1.26/serving** · 135 kcal · P10 · C5 · F9 · ⏱ 55 min · 🍽 6  

A soul-warming Italian-American classic: tender mini meatballs of beef and pork simmered with orzo and wilted greens in a golden, parmesan-rich chicken broth. The name comes from "minestra maritata" — the happy "marriage" of meat and greens.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Ground beef** — for mini meatballs; 80/20
- **Ground pork** — blended with beef for tender meatballs
- **Italian breadcrumbs** — panade base, about 1/2 cup
- **Milk** — to soak breadcrumbs, about 1/4 cup
- **Eggs** — binds the meatballs
- **Parmesan** — half in meatballs, half to finish
- **Fresh parsley** — chopped, for meatballs and garnish
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Onion** — finely diced
- **Carrot** — small dice
- **Celery** — 2 stalks, small dice
- **Chicken broth** — about 8 cups total
- **Orzo** — or acini di pepe, about 3/4 cup dry
- **Spinach** — or escarole, roughly chopped
- **Olive oil** — for searing and sweating veg
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.02 tsp
- **Lemon** — squeeze to brighten at the end _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make the panade: in a large bowl, stir the breadcrumbs with the milk and let sit 5 minutes until pasty. Add ground beef, ground pork, egg, half the parmesan, half the parsley, the minced garlic, 1 tsp salt, and a few grinds of pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until combined — don't overwork it.
2. Roll into small meatballs about 3/4 inch (marble to grape size); you should get 40 or so. Keep them tiny so they cook through in the broth and fit on a spoon.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium-high. Brown the meatballs in batches, turning, just until golden on the outside (about 3 minutes per batch) — they'll finish in the soup. Transfer to a plate.
4. Lower heat to medium. In the same pot, sweat the onion, carrot, and celery in the rendered fat with a pinch of salt for 6 to 8 minutes until softened and fragrant, scraping up the browned bits.
5. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a gentle simmer. Slide the meatballs back in and simmer 10 minutes so they cook through and flavor the broth.
6. Stir in the orzo and cook 8 to 9 minutes, until just tender, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick.
7. Add the chopped spinach (or escarole) and simmer 2 minutes until wilted. Stir in the remaining parmesan.
8. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Finish with a squeeze of lemon if using, ladle into bowls, and top with remaining parsley and extra grated parmesan.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Potato** — about 2 lb russet potatoes, peeled
- **Onion** — 1 medium yellow onion
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (or matzo meal)
- **Salt** — 1.5 tsp kosher salt
- **Black pepper** — 1/2 tsp black pepper
- **Baking powder** — 1/2 tsp, for extra lift and crisp
- **Vegetable oil** — neutral oil for shallow frying, about 1 cup

- **Sour cream** — for serving _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Peel the potatoes. Using the large holes of a box grater (or a food processor shredding disc), coarsely grate the potatoes and the onion together — the onion's enzymes help keep the potato from browning.

6. Fry undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deeply golden brown and crisp at the edges. Adjust the heat so they color steadily without burning.
7. Transfer to a wire rack set over a sheet pan (not paper towels, which steam them soft) and immediately sprinkle with a little salt. Keep warm in a 200F oven while you fry the rest, topping up the oil as needed.
8. Serve hot and fresh with applesauce and sour cream alongside.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Soba noodles** — somyeon (thin Korean wheat noodles); use 2 bundles
- **Gochujang** — Korean red chili paste
- **Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)** — Korean chili flakes for color and gentle heat

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Hot dog** — 2 full-size hot dogs, each cut in half crosswise for 4 dogs
- **Mozzarella** — low-moisture block mozzarella cut into 4 thick batons
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1 cup all-purpose flour for the batter
- **Rice flour** — about 3 tbsp; gives the signature chewy crust
- **Instant yeast** — about 1 tsp
- **Sugar** — about 1.5 tbsp in batter, plus extra for dusting
- **Milk** — warm, about 3/4 cup to bind the batter
- **Eggs** — 1 egg in the batter

**Steps**

6. Dip each skewer into the batter, twirling to coat fully and letting excess drip off, then immediately roll in panko to coat all over.
7. Carefully lower into the oil and fry, turning often, 3-4 minutes until deep golden and the crust is set and crisp; cheese should be just melted inside. Work in small batches so the oil stays hot.

A cozy Korean rice bowl where the rice crisps golden at the bottom while quick-cooked veg, a jammy egg, and a swirl of gochujang pile on top.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Sushi rice** — short-grain or any sticky white rice; crisps best at the bottom
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Carrot** — cut into thin matchsticks
- **Spinach** — 2 big handfuls
- **Mushrooms** — thinly sliced; shiitake if you have it
- **Zucchini** — half a zucchini, thin half-moons _(optional)_
- **Scallion** — sliced, for topping
- **Gochujang** — Korean chili paste; the heart of the bowl
- **Soy sauce** — 1 tbsp
- **Toasted sesame oil** — 1 tsp
- **Garlic** — minced
- **Sugar** — 1 tsp, to balance the gochujang
- **Sesame seeds** — to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Rinse 1 cup rice until the water runs mostly clear, then add it to the rice cooker with 1 cup water and a light brush of vegetable oil around the bottom and sides of the pot (this is what crisps the rice). Start a normal cook cycle.

6. Scoop into bowls, making sure to scrape up some of the crispy bottom rice, and stir it all together just before eating.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose; about 3 cups for 4 manakish
- **Instant yeast** — 2.25 tsp / one packet
- **Sugar** — 1 tsp, to feed the yeast
- **Salt** — 1 tsp in the dough
- **Olive oil** — split: ~3 tbsp in the dough, rest for the za'atar paste
- **Water** — about 1 cup warm water to bring the dough together

**Steps**

6. Spread a generous layer of the za'atar paste over each round, leaving a thin border, pushing it into the dimples right to the edge.
7. Slide onto the hot tray and bake 7-10 minutes, until the base is set and lightly golden but the bread is still soft and pliable; do not overbake or it goes cracker-hard.

#### 🌮 [Beef Barbacoa](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-beef-barbacoa/)
Mexican · dinner · medium  
**$2.53/serving** · 47 kcal · P2 · C9 · F1 · ⏱ 220 min · 🍽 6  

Mexican-style shredded beef braised low and slow until fall-apart tender in a smoky, tangy adobo of chipotle, dried chiles, cumin, clove, and lime. The hallmark of real barbacoa: a tough, well-marbled cut cooked for hours until it pulls apart with a fork, then bathed in its own rich braising liquid. Pile it into warm tortillas with onion and cilantro.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Beef chuck** — boneless chuck roast, cut into large 2-inch chunks
- **Chipotle peppers in adobo** — 3-4 chipotles plus 2 tbsp adobo sauce
- **Ancho chili powder** — stands in for 2 dried ancho chiles
- **Onion** — white onion, quartered for the braise
- **Garlic** — 5 cloves
- **Lime** — juice of 2 limes
- **Apple cider vinegar** — 3 tbsp
- **Beef broth** — about 1.5 cups
- **Cumin** — 1 tbsp ground
- **Oregano** — Mexican oregano, 2 tsp
- **Ground cloves** — 1/4 tsp
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaves
- **Smoked paprika** — 0.5 tsp
- **Salt** — 2 tsp kosher salt, to taste
- **Black pepper** — 0.3 tsp
- **Olive oil** — 2 tbsp for searing
- **Corn tortilla chips** — ignore _(optional)_
- **Tortilla** — warm corn tortillas, for serving
- **Fresh cilantro** — for serving _(optional)_
- **White onion** — finely diced, for serving _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Pat the beef chunks dry and season all over with the salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the beef in batches until deeply browned on all sides, about 8-10 minutes total. Transfer to a plate.
2. Make the adobo: in a blender, combine the chipotles in adobo, ancho chili powder, quartered onion, garlic, lime juice, apple cider vinegar, cumin, Mexican oregano, ground cloves, smoked paprika, and about half the beef broth. Blend until smooth.
3. Return the seared beef and any juices to the pot. Pour the adobo over it, add the remaining broth and the bay leaves, and stir to coat. The liquid should come about two-thirds up the meat.
4. Bring to a gentle simmer, then cover and braise low and slow: either keep at a bare simmer on the stovetop or transfer to a 300F oven. Cook 3 to 3.5 hours, turning the meat once or twice, until completely fork-tender and falling apart.
5. Discard the bay leaves. Lift the beef out and shred it with two forks, removing any large pockets of fat.
6. Return the shredded beef to the pot and toss with the braising liquid. Simmer uncovered 10-15 minutes to concentrate the sauce so the meat stays juicy but not soupy. Taste and adjust salt and lime.
7. Warm the corn tortillas. Serve the barbacoa piled into tortillas, topped with finely diced white onion and chopped cilantro, with extra braising juices spooned over.

</details>

#### 🌮 [Quesabirria Tacos](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/quesabirria-tacos/)
Mexican · dinner · medium  
**$4.58/serving** · 523 kcal · P19 · C79 · F16 · ⏱ 195 min · 🍽 4  

Crispy cheese-fried tacos stuffed with chili-braised beef, served with a rich consommé for dunking.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Beef chuck** — beef chuck roast, cut into large chunks
- **Dried chili** — guajillo chilies, stemmed and seeded
- **Dried chili** — ancho or chiles de arbol for extra heat _(optional)_
- **Onion** — white onion, halved
- **Garlic** — cloves
- **Tomato** — roma tomatoes
- **Beef broth** — cups
- **Cumin** — tsp
- **Oregano** — tsp Mexican oregano
- **Cinnamon** — tsp, or 1 small stick
- **Ground cloves** — tsp
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaf
- **Vinegar** — tbsp apple cider vinegar
- **Salt** — tsp, to taste
- **Black pepper** — tsp
- **Tortilla** — corn tortillas
- **Mozzarella** — cups shredded, low-moisture (sub for Oaxaca)
- **Fresh cilantro** — cup, chopped, for serving
- **Lime** — cut into wedges

**Steps**

1. Toast the dried guajillo chilies in a dry pot for 30 seconds per side, then cover with hot water and soak 10 minutes until soft. Sear the beef chuck chunks with salt and pepper in the pot until browned, then set aside.
2. Blend the soaked chilies with the tomatoes, half the onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, ground cloves, vinegar, and a splash of broth until smooth. Strain the sauce back over the beef.
3. Add the beef broth, bay leaves, remaining onion, and salt. Cover and braise on low for 2.5 to 3 hours (or pressure cook 45 minutes) until the beef shreds easily.
4. Remove the beef, shred it with two forks, and toss with a few ladles of the braising liquid. Skim the orange-red fat off the top of the pot and reserve it for dipping the tortillas. Keep the rest as consommé.
5. Dip each tortilla in the reserved fat, lay it on a hot griddle, and sprinkle with mozzarella. When the cheese melts, add shredded beef to one half and fold the tortilla over.
6. Fry the folded tacos until crispy and golden on both sides, pressing gently. Garnish with chopped cilantro and diced onion.
7. Pour the warm consommé into small bowls with cilantro and a squeeze of lime. Serve tacos hot for dunking.

</details>

Fluffy, tomatoey, garlicky red rice that practically cooks itself in one dump-and-go batch.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Jasmine rice** — long-grain white rice, rinsed (2 cups dry)
- **Tomato sauce** — 1 cup (8 oz) plain tomato sauce
- **Chicken broth** — 2.5 cups; use veggie-broth to keep it vegan
- **Tomato paste** — 1 tbsp for deeper red color and flavor
- **Onion** — half a small onion, finely diced
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Vegetable oil** — 2 tbsp
- **Cumin** — 1 tsp ground cumin
- **Chili powder** — 1 tsp
- **Garlic powder** — 0.5 tsp, optional flavor boost _(optional)_
- **Salt** — 1 tsp, adjust to taste
- **Frozen peas** — 0.5 cup peas and/or diced carrot, optional _(optional)_
- **Fresh cilantro** — 2 tbsp chopped, to finish _(optional)_
- **Lime** — 1 lime, cut into wedges to serve _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs mostly clear, then drain well and add it to the rice cooker pot.
2. Add the tomato sauce, tomato paste, broth, oil, diced onion, minced garlic, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, and salt directly into the pot. Stir so the rice is fully coated and the liquid looks evenly red.

5. Fluff thoroughly with a fork or rice paddle, lifting from the bottom to mix in the deeper-colored rice. If any spots feel underdone, stir, add a splash of broth, and run a short second Cook cycle.
6. Stir in chopped cilantro and serve with lime wedges to squeeze over the top.
