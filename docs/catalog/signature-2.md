#### 🥬 [Microwave Cabbage Ramen Bowl](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/mw-cabbage-ramen-stir-bowl/)
Asian · dinner · easy  
**$1.24/serving** · 436 kcal · P10 · C59 · F16 · ⏱ 6 min · 🍽 1  

Ramen tossed with steamed cabbage and a soy-sesame glaze — high volume for less than a dollar.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Instant ramen** — discard seasoning packet
- **Cabbage** — 1 cup
- **Soy sauce** — 1 tbsp
- **Sesame oil** — 0.5 tsp
- **Garlic powder** — 0.25 tsp
- **Sriracha** — 0.5 tsp _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Shred cabbage and place in a microwave-safe bowl with 1 tbsp water.
2. Cover loosely and microwave 2 minutes until wilted (careful with steam).
3. Add ramen and 1.5 cups water, microwave 3 minutes more.
4. Drain almost all water (leave 2 tbsp), toss with soy, sesame oil, garlic powder, and sriracha.

</details>

#### 🍲 [Microwave Cooker-Style Congee](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/mw-cooker-style-congee/)
Asian · breakfast · easy  
**$1.64/serving** · 215 kcal · P11 · C27 · F6 · ⏱ 7 min · 🍽 1  

A soothing rice porridge built from leftover rice and broth — sick-day food in 6 minutes.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Rice** — 0.5 cup
- **Chicken broth** — 2 cup
- **Ginger paste** — 0.5 tsp
- **Soy sauce** — 0.5 tbsp _(optional)_
- **Scallion** — 1 stalk _(optional)_
- **Eggs** — 1 egg _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Combine rice, broth, and ginger in a deep microwave-safe bowl (use one twice as tall as the liquid line — congee bubbles up).
2. Microwave uncovered 2 minutes, stir, then microwave another 2 minutes.
3. If using egg, crack it in, stir, and microwave 60 more seconds.
4. Stir until creamy, season with soy sauce, and top with scallion (careful with steam).

</details>

#### 🧇 [Belgian Liège Waffles](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-belgian-liege-waffles/)
Belgian · breakfast · medium  
**$0.25/serving** · 74 kcal · P3 · C9 · F3 · ⏱ 155 min · 🍽 6  

Dense, chewy yeasted brioche-dough waffles studded with pearl sugar that melts into crackly caramel pockets against the iron. This is the real Liège-style waffle sold from Belgian street carts — rich, eggy, and sweet enough to eat plain, no syrup required.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — bread flour preferred for chew, ~2.5 cups
- **Instant yeast** — 2.25 tsp / 1 packet instant yeast
- **Whole milk** — lukewarm, ~1/3 cup to bloom yeast
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Butter** — softened, ~10 tbsp, worked into dough
- **Brown sugar** — 2 tbsp light brown sugar in the dough
- **Salt** — 3/4 tsp
- **Vanilla extract** — 1.5 tsp vanilla extract
- **Turbinado sugar** — Belgian pearl sugar (nibsugar) — use turbinado only as a last resort; pearl sugar gives true crunch, ~1.25 cups
- **Cinnamon** — pinch in the dough, traditional touch _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. In a small bowl, stir the instant yeast and a pinch of the brown sugar into the lukewarm milk and let it sit 5-10 minutes until foamy.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the flour and salt. Make a well, then add the eggs, vanilla, remaining brown sugar, and the foamy yeast-milk. Mix into a shaggy, sticky dough.
3. Knead the dough in the bowl for 4-5 minutes until it comes together and is elastic, then add the softened butter a few pats at a time, working each fully in before adding more, until the dough is glossy and smooth.
4. Cover the bowl and let the dough rise in a warm spot for 1.5-2 hours, until doubled and puffy.
5. Gently fold the pearl sugar (and optional cinnamon) into the risen dough by hand — fold, don't overmix, so the sugar stays in distinct pieces. Let it rest 15 minutes.
6. Divide the dough into 6 equal balls (about 110 g each). Meanwhile heat a waffle iron to medium-high; do not oil it, as the sugar self-releases.
7. Place one ball in the center of the iron, close, and cook 3-5 minutes until deep golden brown and the escaping sugar has caramelized at the edges.
8. Carefully lift each waffle out with a fork or wooden spatula and cool on a rack for a minute so the caramel sets and crisps. Eat warm, plain or dusted with powdered sugar.

</details>

#### 🐟 [British Fish and Chips](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-british-fish-and-chips/)

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Cod fillet** — two thick skinless cod fillets, about 180g each
- **Potato** — large Maris Piper or russet potatoes for chips
- **All-purpose flour** — plain flour for the batter, plus a little for dusting
- **Beer (cooking)** — cold lager or pale ale, about 180ml
- **Baking powder** — 1 tsp, for a lighter, crispier batter
- **Vegetable oil** — for deep frying, about 1L
- **Frozen peas** — for mushy peas
- **Butter** — knob, to finish the mushy peas
- **Fresh mint** — few leaves for the mushy peas _(optional)_
- **Lemon** — 0.5 lemon
- **Vinegar** — malt vinegar, for serving
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 1 tsp

**Steps**

1. Peel the potatoes and cut into thick chips about 1.5cm wide. Rinse under cold water to wash off surface starch, then soak in cold water for 10 minutes and pat completely dry.
2. Heat the oil in a deep heavy pan to 130C/265F. Blanch the chips in batches for 6-7 minutes until soft but not coloured, then lift out and drain on a rack. This first fry cooks them through.
3. Make the mushy peas: simmer the frozen peas in a little salted water for 4-5 minutes until tender, drain, then crush roughly with the butter, a few mint leaves, and salt and pepper. Keep warm.

7. Return the blanched chips to the 190C oil and fry for 2-3 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain well and season generously with salt.
8. Serve the fish and chips at once with the mushy peas, lemon wedges, and plenty of malt vinegar.

</details>

#### 🍋 [Lemon Posset](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-lemon-posset/)
British · snack · easy  
**$0.50/serving** · 13 kcal · P0 · C4 · F0 · ⏱ 195 min · 🍽 4  

A silky, set British cream pudding made from just three ingredients — heavy cream, sugar, and fresh lemon. No gelatin, no eggs: the lemon's acid alone thickens the simmered cream into a luxurious, intensely citrusy custard. Topped with fresh berries and mint, it's the easiest elegant dessert you'll ever make.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Heavy cream** — 2 cups (480 ml)
- **White sugar** — 2/3 cup (135 g)
- **Lemon** — for ~6 tbsp fresh juice
- **Lemon zest** — finely grated zest of both lemons
- **mixed-berries** — raspberries or blueberries, to serve _(optional)_
- **Fresh mint** — a few small sprigs to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Finely grate the zest of both lemons, then juice them — you want about 6 tablespoons (90 ml) of fresh juice. Set both aside separately.
2. Pour the heavy cream into a saucepan and add the sugar and lemon zest. Place over medium heat and stir until the sugar fully dissolves.
3. Bring to a gentle boil, then let it boil steadily for exactly 3 minutes, stirring constantly so it doesn't boil over or scorch. This concentrates the cream so it will set firmly.
4. Remove from the heat and let it stand for 1 minute to cool slightly, then whisk in the fresh lemon juice. The acid will immediately begin to thicken the cream.
5. Let the mixture rest off the heat for 5 minutes, then give it a final whisk — it should look smooth and slightly thickened.
6. Pour through a fine strainer into 4 small ramekins, glasses, or jars to catch the zest for a perfectly smooth texture (or leave the zest in for more punch).
7. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate uncovered for at least 3 hours, or until fully set with a soft, custard-like wobble.
8. Just before serving, top each posset with fresh berries and a small sprig of mint.

</details>

A one-pot Louisiana classic: smoky andouille-style sausage and browned chicken thighs simmered with the Cajun "holy trinity," then rice cooked right in the same pot so every grain soaks up the deeply seasoned, slightly browned stock. This is the rustic Cajun "brown" style — no cream, no tomato sauce drowning it out, just real fond, cayenne heat, and bay.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Smoked sausage** — andouille sausage, sliced into coins
- **Chicken thighs** — boneless skinless, cut into chunks
- **Rice** — long-grain white rice, uncooked
- **Chicken broth** — about 3 cups
- **Onion** — 1 onion
- **Bell pepper** — green, for the holy trinity
- **Celery** — 2 stalks
- **Garlic** — 4 cloves, minced
- **Tomato paste** — 1 tbsp, for color and depth
- **Vegetable oil** — 2 tbsp for browning
- **Smoked paprika** — 0.2 tsp
- **Cayenne pepper** — adjust to heat preference
- **Dried thyme** — 0.15 tsp
- **Dried oregano** — 0.1 tsp
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaves
- **Garlic powder** — 0.1 tsp
- **Onion powder** — 0.1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.1 tsp
- **Kosher salt** — to taste
- **Scallion** — sliced, to finish
- **Fresh parsley** — chopped, to finish _(optional)_
- **Hot sauce** — at the table _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make a Cajun seasoning by combining the smoked paprika, cayenne, dried thyme, dried oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and a big pinch of kosher salt. Season the chicken chunks with about half of it.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy Dutch oven or deep skillet over medium-high. Brown the sausage coins until the edges crisp and render fat, 3-4 minutes, then scoop out. Add the chicken and sear until browned on all sides (it doesn't need to cook through), then remove. Leave the dark fond on the bottom of the pot.
3. Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion, green bell pepper, and celery (the holy trinity) and cook, scraping up the browned bits, until softened and starting to color, about 6-8 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste and cook 1 minute until rusty and fragrant.
4. Return the sausage and chicken to the pot with the rest of the Cajun seasoning and the bay leaves. Stir to coat everything.
5. Add the uncooked long-grain rice and stir for a minute so the grains toast slightly and pick up the fat and seasoning.
6. Pour in the chicken broth, scrape the bottom once more, and bring to a boil. Taste the liquid and adjust salt and cayenne now — it should taste assertive.
7. Cover, reduce to low, and simmer undisturbed for 20-25 minutes until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Resist stirring, which makes it gummy.
8. Kill the heat and let it stand covered for 10 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork. Discard the bay leaves, fold in scallions and parsley, and serve with hot sauce on the side.

</details>

#### 🍫 [Chocolate Lava Cake](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-chocolate-lava-cake/)
French · snack · medium  
**$0.62/serving** · 83 kcal · P5 · C3 · F5 · ⏱ 30 min · 🍽 4  

A classic molten chocolate lava cake with a set, fudgy exterior that gives way to a warm, flowing dark-chocolate center. Built the real way — quality dark chocolate and butter melted together, whole eggs plus extra yolks whipped with sugar for lift, and just enough flour to hold the walls. The trick is underbaking: pull them at 12 minutes so the middle stays liquid.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Dark chocolate** — about 6 oz / 170g good bittersweet dark chocolate (60-70%), chopped
- **Butter** — about 10 tbsp unsalted, plus extra for greasing the ramekins
- **Eggs** — 2 whole eggs
- **Eggs** — 2 extra yolks (counted as ~1 egg's worth of cost)
- **Sugar** — about 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- **All-purpose flour** — about 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Salt** — a pinch of fine salt
- **Cocoa powder** — for dusting the buttered ramekins so the cakes release cleanly _(optional)_
- **Powdered sugar** — for dusting before serving _(optional)_
- **Raspberries** — to garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Heat the oven to 425F (220C). Generously butter four 6-oz ramekins, then dust the inside with cocoa powder, tapping out the excess — this is what lets the molten cakes slide out cleanly. Set them on a baking sheet.
2. Combine the chopped dark chocolate and butter in a heatproof bowl. Melt gently over a pan of barely simmering water (or in 30-second microwave bursts), stirring until smooth and glossy. Let it cool for a couple of minutes so it doesn't cook the eggs.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk the 2 whole eggs, 2 extra yolks, sugar, vanilla, and pinch of salt together vigorously for 1-2 minutes, until pale, thickened, and slightly foamy — this whipped air is what gives the cakes their lift.
4. Pour the warm melted chocolate into the egg mixture and whisk until fully combined and uniform.
5. Sift the flour over the top and fold it in gently with a spatula just until no streaks remain. Do not overmix — you want to keep the airiness.
6. Divide the batter evenly among the four ramekins, filling each about three-quarters full.
7. Bake for exactly 11-12 minutes: the tops should be puffed and set with a thin crust, but the centers should still jiggle slightly when nudged. Underbaking is correct — that's the molten core.
8. Let the cakes rest for exactly 1 minute (no longer, or the center keeps cooking). Run a thin knife around each edge, invert a plate over the ramekin, flip together, and lift the ramekin away.
9. Dust with powdered sugar, add a few raspberries, and serve immediately while the center is still flowing.

</details>

#### 🍷 [French Coq au Vin](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-french-coq-au-vin/)

The classic Burgundian braise: bone-in chicken slowly simmered in red wine with smoky lardons, browned mushrooms, and glazed pearl onions. Marinating the chicken in the wine deepens the color and flavor, and a butter-flour beurre manie gives the sauce its glossy, velvety body.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Chicken thighs** — bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs and/or drumsticks
- **Red wine (cooking)** — a dry red Burgundy or Pinot Noir, about 3/4 bottle
- **Bacon** — cut into lardons; slab bacon or pancetta is ideal
- **Cremini mushrooms** — quartered
- **Onion** — small frozen or fresh pearl onions, peeled and kept whole
- **Carrot** — cut into chunks
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, smashed
- **Tomato paste** — about 1 tbsp
- **Chicken broth** — about 1.5 cups, to cover
- **Butter** — half for searing, half for the beurre manie
- **All-purpose flour** — for the beurre manie and to dust the chicken
- **Fresh thyme** — a few sprigs for the bouquet garni
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaf
- **Fresh parsley** — stems in the braise, chopped leaves to finish
- **Olive oil** — for searing
- **Salt** — 0.05 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.03 tsp
- **Bourbon** — stand-in for a splash of brandy/cognac to flambe _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Pat the chicken dry, season generously with salt and pepper, and place in a bowl with the smashed garlic, carrot, thyme, bay leaves, and red wine. 2. Render the bacon lardons in a heavy Dutch oven over medium heat until crisp; remove with a slotted spoon. In the rendered fat plus a little olive oil, brown the pearl onions and mushrooms until golden, then set aside.
3. Dust the dried chicken lightly in flour, then sear skin-side down in the same pot over medium-high heat until deeply browned on both sides, about 8 minutes total. If using, add the bourbon/brandy off the heat and carefully flambe or simmer off.
4. Stir the tomato paste into the pot and cook 1 minute, then pour in the reserved marinade wine and chicken broth, scraping up all the browned bits. Tuck in the carrots, garlic, and a bouquet garni of thyme, bay, and parsley stems.
5. Return the chicken and bacon to the pot, bring to a gentle simmer, then cover and braise on low (or in a 325F oven) for about 45 minutes, until the chicken is tender and reads 175F.
6. Lift out the chicken and keep warm; discard the bouquet garni and skim excess fat from the sauce. Bring the sauce to a brisk simmer to reduce slightly.
7. Mash the remaining soft butter with the remaining flour into a smooth beurre manie, then whisk it into the simmering sauce bit by bit until it lightly coats a spoon.
8. Return the chicken, mushrooms, and pearl onions to the sauce to warm through and glaze, then taste and adjust salt and pepper.
9. Shower with chopped parsley and serve over mashed potatoes, buttered egg noodles, or with crusty bread to soak up the sauce.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Zucchini** — 2 medium zucchini, cut into 3/4-inch half-moons

- **Tomato** — 4 ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped (or sub 1 can crushed)
- **Garlic** — 4 cloves, minced

7. Stir in the olives, then taste and adjust salt and pepper. Discard the bay leaf.

#### 🥞 [Savory Dutch Baby](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-savory-dutch-baby/)

**$1.85/serving** · 163 kcal · P12 · C6 · F10 · ⏱ 35 min · 🍽 2  

A dramatic, oven-puffed pancake baked in a screaming-hot cast-iron skillet until the edges climb the sides and turn golden and crisp. This savory version skips the sugar, folds Gruyere and fresh chives into a custardy egg-and-flour batter, and finishes with a peppery handful of arugula, shaved Parmesan, and a squeeze of lemon. It collapses gracefully into a tender, eggy center the moment it leaves the oven — best torn and shared straight from the pan.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Eggs** — 3 egg
- **Milk** — 3/4 cup whole milk, at room temperature
- **All-purpose flour** — 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- **Butter** — 3 tbsp unsalted butter for the hot skillet
- **Gruyere** — about 1/2 cup grated, stirred into batter
- **Chives** — 2 tbsp finely chopped, plus more to finish
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **Black pepper** — freshly ground
- **Nutmeg** — tiny pinch, optional _(optional)_
- **Arugula** — 2 large handfuls to top
- **Parmesan** — shaved, to finish
- **Lemon** — wedge, to squeeze over
- **Extra virgin olive oil** — drizzle over the greens

**Steps**

1. Set a 10- to 12-inch cast-iron or other oven-safe skillet on the middle rack and preheat the oven to 450F (230C). Let everything else come to room temperature — cold ingredients will not puff well.
2. Crack the eggs into a blender (or a bowl with a whisk) and blend 20 seconds until pale and foamy. Add the room-temperature milk, flour, salt, pepper, and the pinch of nutmeg, then blend just until smooth — about 30 seconds. Let the batter rest 10 minutes; stir in the grated Gruyere and chopped chives.
3. When the oven is fully hot, carefully pull out the skillet, drop in the butter, and swirl until it melts and foams, coating the bottom and sides completely. Work quickly so the pan stays blazing hot.
4. Pour the rested batter into the center of the hot, buttered skillet all at once — do not stir. Return it to the oven immediately.
5. Bake 18 to 22 minutes without opening the door. The Dutch baby will billow up the sides and turn deep golden and crisp at the edges while staying custardy in the center.
6. Meanwhile, toss the arugula with a drizzle of olive oil, a squeeze of lemon, and a small pinch of salt.
7. Pull the puffed pancake from the oven (it will begin to deflate within a minute — that is normal). Pile the dressed arugula into the center, shower with shaved Parmesan and extra chives, and add a final crack of pepper.
8. Cut into wedges and serve immediately, straight from the pan, with a lemon wedge on the side.

#### 🇬🇷 [Greek Pastitsio](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-greek-pastitsio/)
Greek · dinner · hard  
**$2.51/serving** · 317 kcal · P22 · C12 · F20 · ⏱ 135 min · 🍽 8  

The Greek answer to lasagna: long tubes of pasta bound with cheese, a cinnamon-and-clove-scented beef ragù in the middle, and a thick, golden egg-enriched béchamel baked until set. Comforting, aromatic, and deeply satisfying.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **bucatini** — long thick tubular pasta (bucatini or No. 2 macaroni); use spaghetti if unavailable
- **Ground beef** — lean ground beef for the meat sauce
- **Onion** — finely chopped
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves, minced
- **Crushed tomatoes** — one 14 oz can
- **Tomato paste** — 2 tbsp
- **Red wine (cooking)** — 1/2 cup dry red wine for the ragù
- **Cinnamon** — 1 tsp ground, plus a small stick if available
- **Allspice** — 1/2 tsp ground
- **Ground cloves** — pinch, about 1/8 tsp
- **Bay leaves** — 1 leaf
- **Butter** — for the béchamel, about 6 tbsp
- **All-purpose flour** — about 2/3 cup for the roux
- **Milk** — 1 quart whole milk, warmed
- **Eggs** — lightly beaten, to enrich and set the béchamel and pasta layer
- **Parmesan** — stands in for kefalotyri/kefalograviera; grated, split between layers and topping
- **Nutmeg** — freshly grated, for the béchamel
- **Olive oil** — for sautéing the beef and aromatics
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 1 tsp
- **Feta cheese** — crumbled into the pasta layer for extra tang _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Make the ragù: heat olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high, brown the ground beef, breaking it up, then add the chopped onion and garlic and cook until soft. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute, then pour in the red wine and let it bubble away.
2. Add the crushed tomatoes, cinnamon, allspice, ground clove, bay leaf, salt and pepper. Simmer uncovered 25-30 minutes until thick and most of the liquid has cooked off; the sauce should be dry, not soupy. Discard the bay leaf and let it cool slightly.
3. Cook the pasta in well-salted water 1-2 minutes shy of al dente, then drain. Toss the warm pasta with a little olive oil, about a third of the grated cheese, and one beaten egg so the strands don't clump.
4. Make the béchamel: melt the butter over medium heat, whisk in the flour and cook 2 minutes without browning, then add the warm milk in a steady stream while whisking constantly until thick and smooth. Season with salt, pepper and fresh nutmeg, and remove from heat.
5. Temper the remaining 2 eggs with a ladle of hot béchamel, then whisk them back into the sauce along with half of the remaining cheese until glossy and pourable.
6. Assemble in a buttered 9x13 baking dish: spread half the pasta on the bottom, scatter over the optional feta, spoon all the meat ragù over evenly, then top with the remaining pasta. Pour the béchamel over the top, spreading to cover completely, and dust with the last of the cheese.
7. Bake at 350F (175C) for 45-50 minutes until the top is set and deeply golden. Rest at least 20-30 minutes before cutting so the layers firm up into clean squares.

#### 🍛 [Egg Curry (Anda Curry)](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-egg-curry/)

**$2.28/serving** · 286 kcal · P15 · C13 · F20 · ⏱ 45 min · 🍽 4  

Hard-boiled eggs simmered in a rich onion-tomato masala bloomed with whole cumin, ginger-garlic, turmeric, coriander and Kashmiri chili, then finished in coconut milk. A comforting North-Indian classic that's cheap, fast, and deeply savory over rice or with naan.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Eggs** — hard-boiled, 2 per serving
- **Onion** — finely chopped
- **Tomato** — pureed or finely chopped
- **Garlic** — cloves, minced
- **Ginger paste** — 1 tbsp fresh ginger
- **Vegetable oil** — 3 tbsp neutral oil or ghee
- **Cumin** — 1 tsp whole cumin seeds plus ground
- **Turmeric** — 3/4 tsp
- **Coriander (ground)** — 1.5 tsp ground coriander
- **Chili powder** — Kashmiri chili powder for color and mild heat
- **Garam masala** — 1 tsp, added at the end
- **Coconut milk** — half a can, full-fat
- **Fresh cilantro** — small handful, chopped, to finish
- **Salt** — to taste

**Steps**

1. Hard-boil the eggs: cover with water, bring to a boil, then simmer 9 minutes. Cool in cold water, peel, and lightly score or prick each egg so the masala soaks in.
2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium. Add the whole cumin seeds and let them sizzle for 20-30 seconds until fragrant. Optionally fry the peeled eggs in a little oil with a pinch of turmeric until golden, then set aside.
3. Add the chopped onion and cook 8-10 minutes, stirring often, until deeply golden brown. This caramelization is the backbone of the gravy.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and ginger and cook 1-2 minutes until raw smell disappears. Add turmeric, ground coriander, ground cumin, and Kashmiri chili powder; toast 30 seconds with a splash of water so the spices don't burn.
5. Add the pureed tomato and salt. Cook 8-10 minutes until the masala thickens and oil starts to separate at the edges.
6. Pour in the coconut milk plus about 1/2 cup water to loosen to a sauce. Bring to a gentle simmer.
7. Slide in the eggs, spoon gravy over them, cover, and simmer 8-10 minutes so they absorb the flavor.
8. Stir in the garam masala, taste and adjust salt, then finish with chopped cilantro. Rest 5 minutes off heat and serve hot with rice or naan.

</details>

#### 🍚 [Jeera Rice](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-jeera-rice/)

Fragrant North Indian cumin rice: fluffy long-grain basmati studded with whole cumin seeds tempered in ghee until they crackle and turn nutty. A bay leaf, a couple of cloves and a stick of cinnamon perfume the rice as it steams. Simple, aromatic, and the ideal partner to dal, curry, or rajma.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Basmati rice** — long-grain basmati, ~1.5 cups dry
- **Ghee** — 2 tbsp; sub neutral oil to make it vegan
- **Cumin seeds** — 2 tsp whole jeera
- **Bay leaves** — 1 dried bay leaf
- **Whole cloves** — 3 whole cloves
- **Cinnamon** — 1 small stick / 1-inch piece
- **Green cardamom** — 2 lightly crushed green pods _(optional)_
- **Salt** — about 1 tsp, to taste
- **Fresh cilantro** — 2 tbsp chopped, for garnish _(optional)_
- **Water** — about 2.25 cups for the absorption method

**Steps**

1. Rinse the basmati in 2-3 changes of cool water until it runs nearly clear, then soak in fresh water for 20-30 minutes. Drain well in a sieve and let sit a few minutes.
2. Heat the ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds, bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon stick and crushed cardamom; let them sizzle for 20-30 seconds until the cumin darkens slightly and smells nutty and fragrant. Do not let it burn.

6. Turn off the heat and let the rice rest, still covered, for 5 minutes so the grains firm up and finish steaming.
7. Fluff gently with a fork, separating the grains without mashing them. Discard the whole cinnamon, bay leaf and cloves if you like.
8. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve hot alongside dal, rajma, or any curry.

#### 🥭 [Mango Kulfi](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-mango-kulfi/)
Indian · snack · medium  
**$0.80/serving** · 245 kcal · P6 · C32 · F11 · ⏱ 405 min · 🍽 6  

Indian frozen dessert made the traditional way: whole milk slowly reduced until thick and caramel-sweet, enriched with condensed milk, then blended with ripe mango pulp, cardamom, saffron, and crushed pistachios. No churning needed, just freeze in molds for a dense, creamy kulfi with a clean mango finish.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Whole milk** — about 4 cups / 1 liter full-fat milk for reducing
- **Sweetened condensed milk** — about half a 14 oz can
- **Heavy cream** — about 1/2 cup for richness
- **Fresh mango** — ripe Alphonso/Kesar if possible, pureed to ~1 cup pulp
- **Sugar** — about 2 tbsp, adjust to mango sweetness
- **Green cardamom** — seeds from 4-5 pods, finely ground
- **Saffron threads** — a pinch, bloomed in 1 tbsp warm milk _(optional)_
- **Pistachios** — about 3 tbsp, finely chopped, plus extra to garnish
- **Almonds** — about 2 tbsp, finely chopped _(optional)_
- **Cornstarch** — 1 tsp slurry to thicken and prevent ice crystals _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Pour the whole milk into a heavy-bottomed pan and bring to a gentle boil, then lower to a steady simmer. Cook 30-40 minutes, stirring often and scraping the sides down, until reduced by nearly half and thickened. Bloom the saffron in 1 tbsp of the warm milk.
2. Stir in the condensed milk, sugar, ground cardamom, and bloomed saffron. If using, whisk the cornstarch with a splash of cold milk and stir it in, then simmer 3-4 minutes until the base coats the back of a spoon. Remove from heat and let cool fully to room temperature.
3. Peel and chop the ripe mangoes and puree until completely smooth; you want about 1 cup of thick pulp with no chunks. Strain if fibrous.
4. Stir the heavy cream and most of the chopped pistachios and almonds into the cooled milk base, then fold in the mango puree only after the base is cool so the mango stays bright and the dairy does not split.
5. Taste and adjust sweetness, keeping in mind freezing dulls sweetness slightly. Pour the mixture into kulfi molds, popsicle molds, or small paper cups, leaving a little headroom.
6. Top each mold with a few reserved chopped pistachios. Cover with foil and insert sticks through the foil so they stand upright, or seal the molds with their lids.
7. Freeze at least 6-8 hours or overnight until solid. To unmold, dip the mold briefly in warm water for 10-15 seconds, then twist gently to release.
8. Serve straight away, garnished with extra pistachios. For a classic falooda-style plate, slice and serve over soaked vermicelli with a drizzle of rose syrup.

</details>

#### 🍖 [Italian Osso Buco](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-italian-osso-buco/)
Italian · dinner · medium  
**$1.62/serving** · 67 kcal · P2 · C14 · F1 · ⏱ 170 min · 🍽 4  

Milanese braised veal shanks, slow-cooked on the bone in white wine, soffritto, and tomato until the meat is fork-tender and the marrow turns to silk. Finished with a bright lemon-garlic-parsley gremolata. Classic over saffron risotto or polenta.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Beef stew meat** — Ideally 4 cross-cut veal shanks (osso buco), ~1.5 in thick, on the bone
- **All-purpose flour** — for dredging the shanks
- **Olive oil** — 0.06 tbsp
- **Butter** — 0.06 tbsp
- **Onion** — 1 onion
- **Carrot** — 2 carrot
- **Celery** — 2 stalks, finely diced
- **Garlic** — 3 cloves total: some in braise, some for gremolata
- **White wine (cooking)** — 1.5 cups dry white, e.g. Pinot Grigio
- **Crushed tomatoes** — 0.5 can
- **Beef broth** — low-sodium beef or veal stock, ~2 cups
- **Bay leaves** — 2 leaf
- **Fresh thyme** — a few sprigs
- **Salt** — 0.02 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 0.01 tsp
- **Lemon zest** — zest of 1 lemon for gremolata
- **Fresh parsley** — large handful, finely chopped for gremolata

**Steps**

1. Pat the veal shanks dry and season all over with salt and pepper. Tie a loop of kitchen string around the circumference of each so they hold their shape during braising. Dredge lightly in flour and shake off the excess.
2. Heat the olive oil and butter in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the shanks until deeply browned on both flat sides, about 4 minutes per side. Work in batches so they don't steam; transfer to a plate.
3. Lower the heat to medium and add the finely diced onion, carrot, and celery. Cook the soffritto gently, stirring, for 8 to 10 minutes until softened and lightly golden, then stir in the minced garlic for the braise and cook 1 minute more.
4. Pour in the white wine, scraping up all the browned bits from the bottom, and simmer until reduced by about half, 4 to 5 minutes. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, bay leaves, and thyme.
5. Nestle the shanks back in along with any juices and pour in enough broth to come about two-thirds up the meat. Bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Cover and transfer to a 325F oven (or keep at the barest simmer on the stove). Braise for about 2 hours, turning the shanks once halfway, until the meat is fork-tender and pulling from the bone but still holding together.
7. While the meat rests, make the gremolata: finely chop the parsley and remaining garlic together with the lemon zest into a fragrant, bright mince.
8. Remove the shanks, snip off the strings, and keep warm. If the sauce is thin, simmer it uncovered a few minutes to thicken; taste and adjust salt and pepper, and fish out the bay leaves.
9. Spoon the sauce over the shanks, shower generously with gremolata, and serve hot over saffron risotto alla Milanese or soft polenta.

</details>
