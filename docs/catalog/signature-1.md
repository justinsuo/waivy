Big batch of seasoned chicken thighs + rice + broccoli for 4 lunches.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

#### 🍌 [Banana Nice Cream](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/blender-banana-nice-cream/)
American · breakfast · easy  
**$0.46/serving** · 160 kcal · P3 · C30 · F5 · ⏱ 130 min · 🍽 4  

Two-ingredient frozen banana soft-serve, blended until thick and creamy with a splash of milk and vanilla. Tastes like banana ice cream with zero added sugar — just freeze, blend, and scoop.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Banana** — very ripe, peeled, sliced into coins, frozen at least 2 hours
- **Almond milk** — splash to help it blend; add more only if needed
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Peanut butter** — 2 tbsp, stir in or swirl for extra richness _(optional)_
- **Cinnamon** — pinch _(optional)_
- **Dark chocolate chips** — 2 tbsp, fold in after blending _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Peel 4 very ripe bananas, slice into coins, and freeze on a tray for at least 2 hours (or up to overnight) until solid.
2. Add the frozen banana coins, vanilla, and just a splash of almond milk to a blender or food processor.
3. Blend on high, stopping to scrape down the sides every 20-30 seconds. It will look crumbly at first, then suddenly turn smooth and creamy like soft-serve. Add a little more milk only if it won't come together.
4. Blend in the peanut butter and cinnamon if using, then pulse in the chocolate chips so they stay whole.
5. Serve immediately for soft-serve texture, or freeze 1-2 hours for scoopable ice cream.

</details>

#### 🥞 [Blender Pancake Batter](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/blender-pancake-batter/)
American · breakfast · easy  
**$0.68/serving** · 357 kcal · P11 · C46 · F14 · ⏱ 20 min · 🍽 4  

Lump-free, fluffy pancake batter blended in one jar. Toss flour, milk, eggs, and a touch of sugar in a blender, pulse for 20 seconds, and pour straight onto a hot griddle for diner-perfect pancakes every time.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose, scooped and leveled (about 1.5 cups)
- **Milk** — about 1.25 cups; thin with a splash more if needed
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Sugar** — 2 tbsp
- **Baking powder** — 1 tbsp for lift
- **Butter** — 3 tbsp, melted; plus more for the pan
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Salt** — 1/2 tsp _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the milk, eggs, melted butter, vanilla, sugar, and salt to the blender jar first so the wet ingredients sit at the bottom near the blades.
2. Add the flour and baking powder on top, then secure the lid and blend on low for about 15-20 seconds, just until smooth with no dry streaks. Do not over-blend or the pancakes turn tough.
3. Let the batter rest in the jar for 5 minutes so the flour hydrates and the bubbles relax; it should pour like thick cream (loosen with a splash of milk if too thick).
4. Heat a nonstick skillet or griddle over medium and lightly butter it. Pour about 1/4 cup batter per pancake straight from the blender jar.
5. Cook 2-3 minutes until bubbles form across the surface and the edges look set, then flip and cook 1-2 minutes more until golden. Serve warm.

</details>

#### 🍅 [Blender Tomato Soup](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/blender-tomato-soup/)
American · lunch · easy  
**$1.22/serving** · 128 kcal · P3 · C16 · F7 · ⏱ 12 min · 🍽 4  

Velvety, restaurant-style creamy tomato soup made entirely in the blender. Hot broth plus a few minutes of high-speed blending heats and emulsifies canned tomatoes, garlic, and cream into a silky bowl with zero stovetop. Ready in minutes and perfect with grilled cheese.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Crushed tomatoes** — one 28 oz can, fire-roasted if available
- **Vegetable broth** — heated until steaming/boiling
- **Heavy cream** — or half-and-half for a lighter soup
- **Tomato paste** — deepens the tomato flavor
- **Garlic** — cloves
- **Onion** — small, raw; blends smooth
- **Olive oil** — 2 tbsp
- **Sugar** — balances acidity
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Fresh basil** — plus extra for garnish _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Heat the vegetable broth until steaming hot (microwave 3-4 minutes or use boiling water with a bouillon). Hot broth is what warms the finished soup, so don't skip this.
2. Add the crushed tomatoes, hot broth, tomato paste, garlic, raw onion, olive oil, sugar, and salt to the blender. Add the fresh basil if using.
3. Secure the lid (vent the cap and cover with a towel for hot liquid) and blend on low, then ramp to high for 3-4 minutes until completely smooth and steaming.
4. With the blender running on low, pour in the heavy cream and blend 15-20 seconds just to combine.
5. Taste and adjust salt or sugar. Pour into bowls and garnish with torn basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

</details>

#### 🥐 [Cinnamon Rolls](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-cinnamon-rolls/)
American · breakfast · medium  
**$0.14/serving** · 46 kcal · P2 · C6 · F2 · ⏱ 150 min · 🍽 12  

Soft, pillowy yeast rolls swirled with brown sugar and cinnamon, baked until golden and finished with a tangy cream cheese frosting that melts into every layer. The dough is enriched with milk, butter, and egg for that signature tender, fluffy crumb.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **All-purpose flour** — about 4 cups all-purpose flour
- **Whole milk** — 1 cup, warmed to ~110F
- **Instant yeast** — 2 1/4 tsp (one packet)
- **Sugar** — 1/4 cup granulated, for the dough
- **Butter** — 1/3 cup softened for dough + 1/3 cup softened for filling
- **Eggs** — 2 large, room temperature
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Brown sugar** — 3/4 cup packed, for the filling
- **Cinnamon** — 2 1/2 tbsp ground cinnamon
- **Cream cheese** — 4 oz softened, for frosting
- **Powdered sugar** — 1 1/2 cups, for frosting
- **Vanilla extract** — 2 tsp total (dough + frosting)

**Steps**

1. Warm the milk to about 110F (just above body temp), stir in the instant yeast and a pinch of the granulated sugar, and let sit 5 minutes until foamy. In a large bowl whisk together the foamed milk, remaining granulated sugar, eggs, 1/3 cup softened butter, 1 tsp vanilla, and salt.
2. Add the flour about a cup at a time, mixing until a shaggy dough forms, then knead by hand on a floured surface (or with a stand mixer dough hook) for 8-10 minutes until smooth, elastic, and only slightly tacky. Place in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot for about 1 hour, until doubled.
3. Make the filling: mash together the remaining 1/3 cup softened butter, the packed brown sugar, and the cinnamon into a thick spreadable paste.
4. Punch down the risen dough and roll it out on a floured surface into a roughly 16x12 inch rectangle, about 1/4 inch thick.
5. Spread the cinnamon-sugar filling evenly over the dough, leaving a 1/2 inch border along the far long edge. Starting from the long edge nearest you, roll the dough up tightly into a log and pinch the seam to seal.
6. Using unflavored dental floss or a sharp serrated knife, cut the log into 12 even rolls and arrange them cut-side up in a greased 9x13 inch baking dish. Cover loosely and let rise again for 30-45 minutes until puffy and touching.
7. Bake at 350F for 22-28 minutes, until the tops are golden and the centers register about 190F. If they brown too fast, tent loosely with foil.
8. While they bake, beat the softened cream cheese with the remaining 2-3 tbsp softened butter until smooth, then beat in the powdered sugar and remaining 1 tsp vanilla until creamy.
9. Let the rolls cool for 5-10 minutes, then spread the cream cheese frosting over the warm rolls so it melts into the swirls. Serve warm.

</details>

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Ricotta cheese** — whole-milk ricotta, about 1 cup
- **All-purpose flour** — all-purpose, about 1 cup
- **Eggs** — separated
- **Milk** — whole milk, about 3/4 cup
- **Lemon** — zest of both plus 2 tbsp juice
- **Sugar** — about 2 tbsp
- **Baking powder** — 2 tsp
- **Salt** — 1/4 tsp
- **Vanilla extract** — 1 tsp
- **Butter** — for the griddle
- **Maple syrup** — to serve _(optional)_

**Steps**

#### 🧀 [Rice Cooker Mac and Cheese](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/rice-cooker-mac-and-cheese/)
American · dinner · easy  
**$0.89/serving** · 90 kcal · P2 · C3 · F8 · ⏱ 25 min · 🍽 4  

Impossibly creamy, stovetop-free mac and cheese that cooks the pasta and the sauce in one pot right in your rice cooker.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Elbow macaroni** — elbow macaroni, dry (about 8 oz)
- **Water** — start with 2 cups; add a splash more if it looks dry
- **Milk** — whole milk for the creamiest result
- **Evaporated milk** — the secret to a silky, no-grit sauce
- **Butter** — tablespoons
- **Sharp cheddar block** — freshly shredded sharp cheddar (about 8 oz); melts better than pre-shredded
- **American cheese singles** — 2 slices for extra-smooth melt _(optional)_
- **Cornstarch** — teaspoon, tossed with the cheese to prevent clumping
- **Salt** — teaspoon, plus more to taste
- **Black pepper** — teaspoon
- **Mustard powder** — teaspoon; sharpens the cheese flavor _(optional)_
- **Paprika** — teaspoon, for color and warmth _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Add the dry macaroni, water, butter, salt, and pepper to the rice cooker pot and stir once so the pasta is submerged.
2. Close the lid and run a normal cook cycle. After about 8 minutes, open and stir to keep the pasta from sticking; let the cycle continue until most of the water is absorbed and the pasta is just tender, roughly 12-15 minutes total.
3. Toss the shredded cheddar with the cornstarch (and mustard powder, if using) in a bowl so every piece is lightly coated.
4. Pour in the milk and evaporated milk, then add the cheese a handful at a time, stirring after each addition until melted and glossy. Drop in the American singles now if using.
5. If the cooker has switched to Keep Warm, run a short second cook cycle (or hold on Warm with the lid open) and stir constantly for 2-3 minutes until the sauce is thick and creamy.
6. Taste, adjust salt, dust with paprika, and serve immediately while hot and stretchy.

</details>

#### 🥪 [Monte Cristo Sandwich](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-monte-cristo-sandwich/)
American (French-inspired) · lunch · medium  
**$2.14/serving** · 209 kcal · P24 · C7 · F9 · ⏱ 20 min · 🍽 2  

A decadent griddled ham-and-turkey sandwich layered with Gruyère and Dijon, dipped in seasoned egg batter like French toast, pan-fried golden in butter, then dusted with powdered sugar and served with jam. Sweet, savory, and gloriously gooey.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Bread** — 4 slices white sandwich or brioche bread
- **Sliced ham** — thin-sliced deli ham, ~3 oz total
- **Deli turkey** — thin-sliced roast turkey, ~3 oz total
- **Gruyere** — 4 slices Gruyère; Swiss works too
- **Dijon mustard** — thin spread on the inside
- **Eggs** — 2 egg
- **Milk** — about 1/4 cup for the batter
- **Nutmeg** — pinch, warms the batter
- **Salt** — 0.01 tsp
- **Butter** — for griddling
- **Powdered sugar** — for dusting
- **Jam** — raspberry or strawberry, for serving _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, a pinch of nutmeg, and a little salt in a wide shallow dish until fully blended; set aside.
2. Lay out 4 slices of bread. Spread a thin layer of Dijon on the two inner-facing slices.
3. Build two sandwiches: on each, layer a slice of Gruyère, the ham, the turkey, then a second slice of Gruyère so the cheese sits against both bread faces and glues everything together. Press firmly so they hold.
4. Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet or griddle over medium-low heat. Low and slow is key so the cheese melts before the outside burns.
5. Dip each assembled sandwich into the egg batter, letting it soak 5-10 seconds per side until coated but not soggy.
6. Griddle the sandwiches 3-4 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until deep golden brown and the cheese is fully melted through.
7. Transfer to a board and rest 1 minute, then slice each on the diagonal.
8. Dust generously with powdered sugar and serve hot with a small dish of raspberry or strawberry jam for dipping.

</details>

#### 🥩 [Argentine Milanesa](https://justinsuo.github.io/waivy/recipes/cur2-argentine-milanesa/)
Argentine · dinner · medium  
**$2.55/serving** · 95 kcal · P5 · C3 · F7 · ⏱ 55 min · 🍽 4  

The crown jewel of Argentine home cooking: thin beef cutlets marinated in garlicky, parsley-flecked egg, breaded, and shallow-fried until impossibly crisp and golden. Served with lemon wedges to cut the richness. Tender inside, shatteringly crunchy outside.

<details><summary>📋 Ingredients & steps</summary>

**Ingredients**

- **Cube steak** — thin beef milanesa cutlets (nalga/peceto), pounded to 1/4 inch
- **Eggs** — 3 egg
- **Italian breadcrumbs** — fine dry breadcrumbs (pan rallado)
- **Garlic** — cloves, finely minced
- **Fresh parsley** — finely chopped, stirred into the egg
- **Lemon** — cut into wedges for serving
- **Vegetable oil** — for shallow frying
- **Salt** — 1 tsp
- **Black pepper** — 1 tsp
- **Oregano** — pinch in the egg, common in Argentine homes _(optional)_

**Steps**

1. Pat the beef cutlets dry and pound them between sheets of plastic wrap to an even 1/4-inch thickness so they cook fast and stay tender. Season lightly with salt and pepper on both sides.
2. In a wide shallow dish, beat the eggs with the minced garlic, chopped parsley, a pinch of oregano, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and several grinds of pepper. For the best flavor, submerge the cutlets in this egg mixture and let them marinate 30 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge).
3. Spread the breadcrumbs in a second wide dish. Lift each cutlet from the egg, letting excess drip off, then press firmly into the breadcrumbs on both sides, packing the crumbs on so the entire surface is fully coated. Press a second time for a thick crust.
4. Pour oil into a large skillet to a depth of about 1/4 inch and heat over medium-high until shimmering (a breadcrumb should sizzle on contact, around 350F).
5. Fry the milanesas one or two at a time, without crowding, for about 2 to 3 minutes per side until deep golden brown and crisp. Flip only once.
6. Transfer to a wire rack or paper-towel-lined plate to drain; never stack them or the crust softens. Sprinkle immediately with a little flaky salt.
7. Serve hot with lemon wedges to squeeze over the top, alongside a green salad or mashed potatoes.

</details>
